# Polarization

> *Two parts, each certain it is the only thing standing between you and catastrophe, and each pointing at the other one.*

---

## The Locked Pair

Polarization is [[Internal Family Systems]]' name for two parts caught in escalating opposition, each holding an extreme position *because* the other holds its opposite. The signature is that neither part's extremity makes sense alone. The restricting part is as rigid as it is because the bingeing part is as wild as it is, and the bingeing part is as wild as it is because the restriction is so total. The workaholic [[Managers|manager]] drives harder because the collapse-on-the-couch [[Firefighters|firefighter]] keeps seizing whole weekends; the collapse deepens because the driving never stops. Binge and restrict, work and collapse, the rager and the appeaser, the part that reaches for people and the part that slams the door, every polarized pair runs the same escalator. Each part experiences the other as the emergency, treats every inch it might yield as an inch the enemy gains, and therefore cannot soften first. The war is symmetrical, and both soldiers believe they are the last line of defense.

What the combatants never see is what they agree on. Polarized parts almost always guard the *same* [[Exiles|exile]], they are rival strategies for the same unbearable cargo, opposite answers to one question. The seesaw is not chaos; it is two protectors overcorrecting for each other around a wound neither can heal.

---

## Stability Through War

The counterintuitive part is that polarization *works*, in a grim way, the standoff stabilizes the system. As long as the two protectors are locked on each other, attention stays on the battle and off the [[Burdens|burden]] beneath it, and each extreme reliably cancels the other's worst excesses. This is why the pattern is so durable, and why siding with one pole always fails. The person, usually identified with the more respectable manager, enlists willpower for restriction or productivity, which the opposite part correctly reads as escalation and answers in kind. You cannot win a polarization by joining it. Every diet that ended in a binge has already demonstrated the theorem.

---

## Depolarization

Depolarization is diplomacy, and only [[Self (IFS)|Self]] can chair the talks, because Self is the one presence in the system with no stake in either pole. The work is to get to know each part separately, usually via [[The Six Fs]], until both trust that they have been heard, then let them discover the thing that dissolves the war: they want the same outcome and guard the same child. Parts that meet in the presence of [[Self-Energy]] tend to negotiate like exhausted veterans offered an honest broker. The lasting peace, though, comes from [[Unburdening]] the exile they share, after which neither extreme job is needed and both parts are free to shrink back to their natural size. [[Parts Mapping]] often makes a polarization visible before the person can feel it, two entries on the page glaring at each other. The far side of that war is a balance held on purpose: opposites that contain one another instead of escalating, which is roughly the peace depolarization is trying to broker.

Jung mapped the same dynamic at archetypal scale: [[Enantiodromia]], the lawlike flip of any extreme into its opposite, the psyche's own pendulum. IFS domesticates the principle, the swing between opposites is not fate but a relationship between two parts, and relationships can be mediated.
