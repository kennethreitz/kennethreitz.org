# Anxiety

> *The threat-detection system running at full power with no threat in the room.*

---

## Overview

**Anxiety** is the mind's alarm firing without a fire. It is the same machinery that keeps us alive in genuine danger, sharpened attention, a racing heart, a body braced to fight or flee, running on a future that hasn't happened and may never. Where it protects, it is a gift. Where it persists past usefulness, it becomes a condition in its own right, the most common class of psychiatric disorder.

## Anxiety Versus Fear

The cleanest distinction is the object. **Fear** has one: a specific, present threat, the car swerving, the dog lunging. It is concrete and it resolves when the threat passes. **Anxiety** is anticipatory and diffuse. It points at what *might* happen, often without a name, and so it has no natural off-switch. Fear ends; anxiety waits.

## The Body's Alarm

Underneath sits **sympathetic arousal**, the autonomic surge of adrenaline and cortisol that quickens the pulse, shortens the breath, tightens the gut, and floods the muscles. This is adaptive in a real emergency and exhausting when it never stands down. The physical symptoms are not "in your head"; they are a nervous system behaving exactly as designed, just at the wrong moment.

## The Forms

Anxiety wears several faces. **Generalized anxiety** is free-floating worry that latches onto whatever is nearest. **Panic** is the alarm firing all at once, discrete attacks of terror with crushing physical symptoms. **Social anxiety** centers on the threat of being judged or exposed. **Phobias** attach the whole apparatus to a single trigger. Most share a cognitive engine: **catastrophizing**, the loop where the mind rehearses the worst outcome until rehearsal feels like prophecy.

## Treatment

The first line is **CBT**: naming the catastrophic loop and testing it against reality, paired with **exposure**, deliberately approaching the feared thing until the alarm recalibrates. Medication supports this. **Benzodiazepines** act fast but court tolerance and **dependence**, which is why clinicians increasingly reach for benzo-sparing options. **Gabapentin** is one such choice, it dampens the anxious arousal without the addictive liability.
