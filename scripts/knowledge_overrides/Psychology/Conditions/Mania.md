# Mania

> *The engine is still running, it's just running so far past the redline that the gauge no longer means anything.*

---

## Overview

**Mania** is the high pole of [[Bipolar Disorder]], a sustained state of abnormally elevated, expansive, or **irritable** mood paired with a surge of energy and goal-directed activity. Clinically it requires at least a week of disturbance (or any duration if hospitalization is needed) and marks the difference between bipolar I and the milder forms. It is not happiness turned up; it is the brain's throttle stuck open, the whole system overclocked until function inverts into harm.

## The Manic Pole

The signature is a **decreased need for sleep**: three hours feels sufficient, sometimes none feels necessary, and the deprivation itself accelerates the cycle. **Racing thoughts** outrun the mouth, producing **pressured speech** that is fast, loud, and hard to interrupt. **Grandiosity** inflates the sense of self into special powers, missions, or destinies, while **distractibility** scatters attention across a dozen unfinished aims.

Then comes the **impulsivity** that does the real damage: spending sprees, reckless driving, sexual indiscretion, abandoned commitments. At the severe end, the floor drops out into **psychosis**, delusions and hallucinations, which is where mania overlaps with [[Schizoaffective Disorder]].

## Hypomania and the Seduction

**Hypomania** is the same engine at a lower RPM: noticeably elevated and productive, lasting at least four days, but without psychosis or the wreckage that forces hospitalization. This is the seductive zone. Work feels effortless, ideas connect, and output spikes, which is exactly what masks the danger. The productivity is real, but it is borrowed against the **crash** that follows: the swing down into [[Depression]], emptied out, often ashamed of what the manic self set in motion.
