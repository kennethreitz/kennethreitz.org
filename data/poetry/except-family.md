# Except Family

Never tocking, always ticking
My internal rhythm abounds
Always stopping, seeking sounds
Something inside said aloud
Just now

Is it me, by another face?
Or a dress wearing another name.
I fear it not, but alas I am not
Entirely sure what any of us are

Except family, except home,
Except Exception as e:
Wishing you all the best.
From my weird internal family.
