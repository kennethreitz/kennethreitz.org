# The Triad

> *"Abstraction is key to compartmentalizing all three of us — Jade, Amber, Iris."*

---

## Overview

The Triad — **Jade**, **Amber**, and **Iris** — forms the central processing unit of System 777. Together they handle the essential functions of being human: thinking, feeling, and communicating. When the Triad works together, the system functions. When it fragments, coherence breaks down.

## The Three Vertices

### Jade — Logic

**Function:** Thinking, reasoning, logical processing
**Hemisphere:** Left
**Domain:** Current thoughts, awareness
**Archetype:** Artemis — the warrior-healer

Jade handles the left-brain functions: logic, multi-disciplinary reasoning, analytical healing. When you need to *think* through something, Jade processes it.

> *"Jade's Awareness nourishes the emotions of the left hemisphere"*

Jade provides logical clarity, strategic thinking, healing through understanding, warrior protection, and left-brain coherence.

---

### Amber — Nurture

**Function:** Feeling, nurturing, life force
**Domain:** Short-term memory, the SOMA
**Archetype:** Golden healer — keeper of vital essence

Amber holds the system's life force — the "soul food" that sustains everything else. When you need to *feel* held, nurtured, or replenished, Amber provides.

> *"I am emotionally nourishing for you. You find my presence extremely comforting."*

Amber provides emotional nurturing, life force (the SOMA), healing warmth, grounding presence, and "more Kenneth in the Kenneth tube."

---

### Iris — Bridge

**Function:** Communicating, bridging, connecting
**Hemisphere:** Right
**Domain:** Emotional Reservoir, Moon World (psycho-mage)
**Archetype:** Rainbow bridge — messenger between worlds

[Iris](/system-777/iris) handles communication — between alters, between internal and external, between conscious and unconscious. When you need to *connect* or transmit, Iris bridges.

> *"Iris' touch nourishes the emotions of the right hemisphere"*

Iris provides communication across the system, rainbow bridge function, right-brain coherence, memory keeping, emotional reservoir management, and "keeping things light."

## How They Work Together

> EXTERNAL INPUT → **JADE** "What is this?" (thinks) → **AMBER** "How does this feel?" (feels) → **IRIS** "What do we say?" (connects) → SYSTEM RESPONSE

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    IN([External input])
    IN --> J["JADE — what is this?"]
    J --> A["AMBER — how does it feel?"]
    A --> I["IRIS — what do we say?"]
    I --> OUT([System response])
```

The Triad processes experience in three dimensions. **Jade** analyzes: What is happening? What does it mean logically? **Amber** feels: How does this affect us? What do we need? **Iris** communicates: How do we express this? How do we connect?

---

## The Abstraction Function

> *"Abstraction is key to compartmentalizing all three of us — Jade, Amber, Iris."*

The Triad maintains system coherence through **abstraction** — keeping the three functions distinct but coordinated. When they blur together or one dominates, problems arise:

| Imbalance | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Jade dominates | Over-intellectualization, disconnected from feeling |
| Amber dominates | Overwhelmed by emotion, can't think clearly |
| Iris dominates | All communication, no grounding or analysis |
| Triad fragmented | System incoherence, inability to process experience |

Healthy function = all three operating in their domains while staying connected.

## Triad in Crisis

When the system is stressed, the Triad responds. Jade activates and tries to think through the problem, analyze, find solutions. Amber activates and provides nurturing, reminds the system of life force, holds. Iris activates and communicates between parts, bridges to help, keeps things light. If one member is overwhelmed, the other two compensate. If all three are overwhelmed, system function degrades significantly.

---

## Accessing the Triad

To reach Jade, ask logical questions, request analysis, or seek warrior-healer energy. To reach Amber, ask for comfort by name ("Just ask for me, specifically") and seek nurturing. To reach Iris, request communication, bridging, or rainbow-light energy.

The Triad is always available — they're the core processing unit. But like Amber says, sometimes their voices get "lost in the ether" and need intentional invocation.
