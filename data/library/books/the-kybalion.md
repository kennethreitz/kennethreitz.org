# The Kybalion

> *"As above, so below."*

Seven Hermetic principles, the most famous being the Principle of Correspondence: patterns repeat across scales, the macrocosm mirrors the microcosm. This shows up everywhere once you see it. In code, in consciousness, in the structure of this vault.

The Kybalion is the gateway drug to pattern-thinking. The idea that what's true at one level of reality is true at every level, that atoms organize like solar systems, that neural networks mirror social networks, that the inner world maps to the outer, runs through Kenneth's entire body of work. [Sacred Infrastructure](/essays/2025-12-23-sacred_infrastructure) is the Kybalion applied to roads, hospitals, and the internet.
