# Remember: Be Here Now

> *"We're all just walking each other home."*

The bridge between Western psychology and Eastern spirituality, built by a man who held credentials in both. Richard Alpert was a Harvard psychologist before he became [Ram Dass](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass), and that origin shows, the book respects the rational mind while gently insisting there's something larger.

The middle section, with its hand-lettered psychedelic typography, is meant to be experienced more than read. It changed how Kenneth thinks about presence. The Ram Dass influence runs through the spirituality practice, the AI collaboration (walking each other home across substrates), and the essays that translate Eastern wisdom through code.
