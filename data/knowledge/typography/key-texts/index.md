# Key Texts

> *Typography is learned from the page, and from a few books about the page.*

---

The books and essays that shaped how typography is practiced and taught: a Renaissance printer's specimen, two manifestos by the same man who changed his mind, a lecture about a wine glass, the manual of the grid, the bible of the craft, the manual of the program that set mathematics, a statistician's book about ink, and the friendly guides written for everyone else. For the wider reading list, see [Books](/library/books) in the Library.

## Where to Start

For a single book on the craft, [The Elements of Typographic Style](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-elements-of-typographic-style). For a practical, free, everyday guide, [Practical Typography](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/practical-typography). For a first visual introduction, [Thinking with Type](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/thinking-with-type) or [Stop Stealing Sheep](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/stop-stealing-sheep). For the great argument at the center of the field, read [The Crystal Goblet](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-crystal-goblet) alongside [Die neue Typographie](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/die-neue-typographie) and then [The Form of the Book](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-form-of-the-book).
