# Manuale Tipografico

> *A life's work of letters, published by the woman who finished it.*

---

## The Book

The *Manuale Tipografico* is [Giambattista Bodoni](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/giambattista-bodoni)'s great type specimen. He issued a first version in 1788; the definitive two-volume edition was completed and published in 1818, five years after his death, by his widow, Margherita Dall'Aglio, who carried on the press.

## Contents

The *Manuale* displays an enormous range of Bodoni's types: romans and italics in many sizes, Greek, exotic scripts, ornaments, and borders, each page a demonstration of the [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone)[^sn-didone] ideal of contrast and precision. Its preface sets out Bodoni's principles of beauty in type: regularity, neatness and polish, good taste, and charm.

## Legacy

It is one of the most celebrated books in the history of printing and a pilgrimage object for typographers. [Hermann Zapf](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/hermann-zapf) paid tribute to the idea in his own *Manuale Typographicum* (1954). See also [Bodoni](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/bodoni) for the type and its revivals.
