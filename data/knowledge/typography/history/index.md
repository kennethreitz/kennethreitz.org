# History

> *Five and a half centuries of making letters repeatable.*

---

The history of typography is a history of technologies, each changing what letters could look like and who could set them: hand-set metal type, the casting machines of the industrial age, photographic type, the personal computer, and the web. Running through it are the movements that argued about what type should be, from the private presses to the Bauhaus to the Swiss grid.

---

## The Eras

| Era | Period | Where | Technology |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Movable Type](/knowledge/typography/history/movable-type) | 1040s (China); 1440s to 1450s (Europe) | China, Korea, Mainz | Individual reusable type |
| [The Aldine Press](/knowledge/typography/history/the-aldine-press) | 1490s to 1590s | Venice | Hand press; Griffo's roman, italic, and Greek |
| [Arts and Crafts and the Private Press](/knowledge/typography/history/arts-and-crafts-and-the-private-press) | 1880s to 1930s | England | Hand press, revived historic types |
| [Hot Metal Typesetting](/knowledge/typography/history/hot-metal-typesetting) | 1880s to 1970s | United States and Britain | Linotype and Monotype casting machines |
| [Bauhaus and the New Typography](/knowledge/typography/history/bauhaus-and-the-new-typography) | 1919 to 1933 | Germany | Metal type, photography, asymmetric layout |
| [Swiss International Style](/knowledge/typography/history/swiss-international-style) | 1950s to 1970s | Switzerland (Basel and Zurich) | Grid-based layout, neo-grotesque type |
| [Phototypesetting](/knowledge/typography/history/phototypesetting) | 1950s to 1980s | Europe, United States | Film negatives exposed by light |
| [Desktop Publishing](/knowledge/typography/history/desktop-publishing) | 1984 to 1990s | United States, then worldwide | Personal computers, PostScript, laser printers |
| [Web Fonts](/knowledge/typography/history/web-fonts) | 1990s onward | The web | CSS @font-face, WOFF, font services |
| [Variable Fonts](/knowledge/typography/history/variable-fonts) | 2016 onward | Industry-wide | OpenType 1.8 font variations |
| [Incunabula](/knowledge/typography/history/incunabula) | c. 1450 to 1500 | Europe | Hand press, metal type |
