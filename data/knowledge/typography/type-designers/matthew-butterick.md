# Matthew Butterick

> *Typography is the visual component of the written word.*

---

## The Typographer

Matthew Butterick is an American typographer, type designer, lawyer, and programmer. He wrote *Typography for Lawyers* (2010) and then the free web book [Practical Typography](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/practical-typography),[^sn-practical-typography] which became one of the most read guides to typography on the internet. His typefaces, including Equity, Concourse, and the monospaced Triplicate, are sold directly to readers.

## Programmer

Butterick built his books with Pollen, a publishing system he wrote in the Racket programming language, an example of the old idea that a typographer should control the whole process, from text to page, which links him to [Donald Knuth](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/donald-knuth) and TeX.

## Ideas

His core argument is simple and pointed: most documents are typeset badly by default, and a handful of decisions (point size, line length, [Leading](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/leading), better fonts than [Times New Roman](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/times-new-roman), correct [Quotes and Dashes](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/quotes-and-dashes)) fix most of the problems. It is typography for humans, very close in spirit to [For Humans](/knowledge/philosophy/for-humans).
