# Edward Tufte

> *Above all else show the data.*

---

## The Information Designer

Edward Tufte is a statistician and political scientist, professor emeritus at Yale, who became the most influential writer on the visual display of information. His first book, [The Visual Display of Quantitative Information](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-visual-display-of-quantitative-information) (1983), was self-published through his Graphics Press so that he could control every detail of its production, and it was followed by *Envisioning Information* (1990), *Visual Explanations* (1997), and *Beautiful Evidence* (2006).

## Ideas

Tufte coined or popularized many terms now standard in design: *chartjunk* for decoration that adds nothing; the *data-ink ratio*; *small multiples*; and *sparklines*, word-sized graphics embedded in text. His books integrate text and image tightly, with notes in wide margins rather than at the foot of the page, the origin of the sidenote style used in Tufte CSS.

## Typography

His books are set in [ET Book](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/et-book),[^sn-et-book] a [Bembo](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/bembo)-based face he released freely. Tufte's principles of clarity and economy shape KJVstudy.org and the sidenote conventions of this vault's essays (see [Building for Contemplation When Spiritual Practice Becomes Code](/essays/2025-11-23-building_for_contemplation_when_spiritual_practice_becomes_code)).
