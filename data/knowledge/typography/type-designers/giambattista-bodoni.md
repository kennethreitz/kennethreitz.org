# Giambattista Bodoni

> *The king of typographers, and the typographer of kings.*

---

## The Printer

Giambattista Bodoni trained at the press of the Propaganda Fide in Rome, which printed in dozens of exotic scripts, and in 1768 became director of the ducal printing house in Parma. He spent the rest of his life there, printing luxurious editions for an international clientele and cutting a vast number of types.

## The Style

Bodoni's types moved from the [Transitional](/knowledge/typography/classification/transitional)[^sn-transitional] manner of [John Baskerville](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/john-baskerville) and Fournier toward the extreme contrast, vertical stress, and hairline [serifs](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/serif) of the [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone), which he developed in parallel with the [Didots](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/firmin-didot) in Paris. His pages are large, spare, and dramatic, monuments to the letterforms themselves (see [Bodoni](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/bodoni)).

## The Manual

His widow published his [Manuale Tipografico](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/manuale-tipografico) in 1818, an enormous specimen of his types with his principles of typography, which put regularity, neatness, good taste, and charm at the heart of the craft.
