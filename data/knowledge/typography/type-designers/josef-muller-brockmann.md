# Josef Müller-Brockmann

> *The grid is a discipline, not a cage.*

---

## The Designer

Josef Müller-Brockmann was a Zurich graphic designer and teacher, and one of the defining figures of the [Swiss International Style](/knowledge/typography/history/swiss-international-style). His concert posters for the Zurich Tonhalle, built from sans serif type and abstract geometric forms arranged on rigorous grids, are among the most celebrated works of modernist graphic design.

## The Grid

He was a co-founder of the journal *Neue Grafik* (1958), which carried the Swiss approach abroad, and his book [Grid Systems in Graphic Design](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/grid-systems-in-graphic-design) (1981) remains the classic manual of the method (see [Grid Systems](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/grid-systems)). For Müller-Brockmann, the grid was an ethical tool as well as a practical one: it made design objective, clear, and accountable to the reader.

## Type

He favored grotesque and neo-grotesque faces, above all [Akzidenz-Grotesk](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/akzidenz-grotesk)[^sn-akzidenz-grotesk] and later [Helvetica](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/helvetica), set flush left and ragged right with careful [hierarchy](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/typographic-hierarchy).
