# Type Designers

> *Behind every typeface is a hand, and usually a long argument.*

---

The people who made Western typography: the inventor of the press, the punchcutters of Venice and Paris, the founders of London and Birmingham, the printers of Parma and Paris, the reformers of the Arts and Crafts movement, the modernists of Germany and Switzerland, the designers who carried type through photosetting into the digital age, and the writers who taught everyone else how to use it. Some of them, like [Eric Gill](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/eric-gill), left difficult legacies, and their notes say so.

## Lines of Influence

Some of the clearest threads: [Johannes Gutenberg](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/johannes-gutenberg) to [Nicolas Jenson](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/nicolas-jenson) to [Aldus Manutius](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/aldus-manutius) and [Francesco Griffo](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/francesco-griffo) to [Claude Garamond](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/claude-garamond), the making of the roman; [William Caslon](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/william-caslon) to [John Baskerville](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/john-baskerville) to [Giambattista Bodoni](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/giambattista-bodoni) and [Firmin Didot](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/firmin-didot), the road to the [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone); [William Morris](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/william-morris) to [Edward Johnston](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/edward-johnston) to [Eric Gill](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/eric-gill), the English revival; [Paul Renner](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/paul-renner) and [Jan Tschichold](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/jan-tschichold) to [Josef Müller-Brockmann](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/josef-muller-brockmann), [Max Miedinger](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/max-miedinger), and [Adrian Frutiger](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/adrian-frutiger), the modernists; and [Hermann Zapf](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/hermann-zapf) to [Donald Knuth](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/donald-knuth), the meeting of calligraphy and computing. The writers, [Beatrice Warde](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/beatrice-warde), [Stanley Morison](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/stanley-morison), [Edward Tufte](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/edward-tufte), [Robert Bringhurst](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/robert-bringhurst), [Erik Spiekermann](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/erik-spiekermann), and [Matthew Butterick](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/matthew-butterick), are collected with their books in [Key Texts](/knowledge/typography/key-texts).
