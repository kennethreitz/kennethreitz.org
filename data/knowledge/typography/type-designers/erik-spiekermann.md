# Erik Spiekermann

> *Typography is the visible voice of a sentence; he wanted it to speak clearly in the street.*

---

## The Designer

Erik Spiekermann is a German type and information designer who co-founded the design firm MetaDesign (1979) and the type distributor FontShop (1989), whose FontFont library became one of the most important collections of the early digital era.

## The Work

His FF Meta (1991) grew from a typeface proposed for the West German post office in the 1980s; it became one of the defining [Humanist Sans](/knowledge/typography/classification/humanist-sans)[^sn-humanist-sans] faces of the 1990s, sometimes called "the Helvetica of the nineties." He designed ITC Officina, wayfinding systems for Berlin's public transport, and corporate type for Deutsche Bahn, among much else.

## The Writer

With E. M. Ginger he wrote [Stop Stealing Sheep](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/stop-stealing-sheep) (1993), a friendly introduction to how type works for people who had suddenly acquired fonts with [Desktop Publishing](/knowledge/typography/history/desktop-publishing). In later years he returned to letterpress printing in Berlin.
