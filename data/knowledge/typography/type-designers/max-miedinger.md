# Max Miedinger

> *A typesetter and salesman who drew the most famous sans serif of the century.*

---

## The Designer

Max Miedinger trained as a typesetter in Zurich and studied at the Zurich school of arts and crafts. He worked for the Haas Type Foundry in Münchenstein, near Basel, as an in-house designer and representative before going freelance.

## Helvetica

In 1956 Eduard Hoffmann, director of Haas, commissioned him to draw a new sans serif to compete with Berthold's [Akzidenz-Grotesk](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/akzidenz-grotesk),[^sn-akzidenz-grotesk] which was then the favorite of Swiss designers. Working closely with Hoffmann, Miedinger produced *Neue Haas Grotesk*, released in 1957 and renamed [Helvetica](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/helvetica) in 1960 for international sale.

## Legacy

Miedinger designed few other faces, and Helvetica's fame far outstripped his own. His work is the clearest example of the [Neo-Grotesque](/knowledge/typography/classification/neo-grotesque) ideal of the [Swiss International Style](/knowledge/typography/history/swiss-international-style): a face made to be neutral, so that the design around it could speak.
