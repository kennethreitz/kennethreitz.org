# Jonathan Hoefler

> *A foundry built on the idea that a typeface is a whole family of decisions.*

---

## The Designer

Jonathan Hoefler founded the Hoefler Type Foundry in New York in 1989. His Hoefler Text (1991), a richly featured [Old-Style](/knowledge/typography/classification/old-style)[^sn-old-style] family, shipped with Apple's Macintosh system software and showed what a digital text face could contain. His HTF Didot (1991) for Harper's Bazaar revived the [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone) in multiple optical sizes (see [Didot](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/didot)).

## Hoefler & Frere-Jones

In 1999 [Tobias Frere-Jones](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/tobias-frere-jones) joined him, and the firm became Hoefler & Frere-Jones, producing some of the most widely used typefaces of the 2000s, including [Gotham](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/gotham), Whitney, Mercury, and Archer. The partnership ended in 2014 in a lawsuit over Frere-Jones's claimed share of the company, which was later settled.

## Hoefler & Co.

The company continued as Hoefler & Co., releasing families like Operator and its coding companion [Operator Mono](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/operator-mono), and was acquired by Monotype in 2021.
