# Typefaces

> *Each typeface is a decision about how a language should look.*

---

A selection of the typefaces that matter most, either historically or in Kenneth's own work: the Renaissance and Enlightenment book faces, the nineteenth-century display faces, the modernist sans serifs, the screen faces of the early web, and the faces that set this vault itself, [Latin Modern](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/latin-modern) for reading and [IBM Plex](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/ibm-plex) Mono for writing. Each note gives the face's designer, date, family, and maker, and links to the people in [Type Designers](/knowledge/typography/type-designers) and the families in [Classification](/knowledge/typography/classification).
