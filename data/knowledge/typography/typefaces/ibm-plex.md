# IBM Plex

> *A corporate typeface that gave itself away.*

---

## The Face

IBM Plex is IBM's corporate type family, designed under Mike Abbink at IBM with the Dutch foundry Bold Monday and released in 2017 under the open SIL Open Font License. It replaced Helvetica Neue as IBM's brand face, and its openness made it one of the most widely used free families in software.

## The Family

Plex is a superfamily: Sans, Sans Condensed, Serif, and Mono, plus scripts for many languages. The Sans is a [Grotesque](/knowledge/typography/classification/grotesque) with distinct engineered details, such as squared-off curves that recall IBM's industrial heritage. The Mono keeps those details in a fixed-width design that is very comfortable for code and prose alike (see [Monospace](/knowledge/typography/classification/monospace)).

## In This Vault

IBM Plex Mono is the writing face of the Reitz LaTeX theme: headings, bold, and italic all in one honest monospace, in the spirit of iA Writer, before Reading view swaps to [Latin Modern](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/latin-modern) (see [Draft to Document Why I Made My Notes Look Like LaTeX Papers](/essays/2026-11-04-draft_to_document)). The same Plex Mono is also the ancestor of iA's own open fonts, including [iA Writer Duo](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/ia-writer-duo).[^sn-ia-writer-duo]
