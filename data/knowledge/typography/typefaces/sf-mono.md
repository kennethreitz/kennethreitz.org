# SF Mono

> *The San Francisco family, given a fixed-width cell for the Terminal.*

---

## The Face

SF Mono is the [Monospace](/knowledge/typography/classification/monospace)[^sn-monospace] member of Apple's San Francisco family (see [SF Pro](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/sf-pro)), introduced in 2016 for the Terminal, Console, and Xcode. It shares San Francisco's neutral, slightly rounded [Neo-Grotesque](/knowledge/typography/classification/neo-grotesque) character, adapted to a fixed-width grid.

## Character

SF Mono has a generous [X-Height](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/x-height), clear distinctions between commonly confused characters, and a range of weights, which lets syntax highlighting use weight as well as color. It is designed to sit comfortably next to SF Pro in interfaces where code and prose mix.

## Availability

Apple ships SF Mono with its developer tools and makes it available for development on Apple platforms; its license does not permit general use elsewhere, which is why open alternatives like [IBM Plex](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/ibm-plex) Mono and [Iosevka](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/iosevka) are common on other systems.
