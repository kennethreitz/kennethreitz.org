# SF Pro

> *Apple's own voice, sized automatically to the moment.*

---

## The Face

San Francisco is Apple's in-house typeface family. It first appeared on the Apple Watch, announced in 2014, and in 2015 replaced Helvetica Neue as the system font of iOS and OS X. The main family is now called SF Pro, with relatives including SF Compact (for the Watch), SF Pro Rounded, New York (a serif), and [SF Mono](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/sf-mono).

## Optical Sizes and Tracking

SF Pro was built for screens at every scale. It comes in Text and Display optical sizes, with the system switching between them around 20 points, and the system applies size-specific [Tracking](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/tracking) automatically: small text is spaced more loosely, large text more tightly. The family later became a [variable font](/knowledge/typography/history/variable-fonts) with an optical size axis that makes the transition continuous.

## Character

A [Neo-Grotesque](/knowledge/typography/classification/neo-grotesque) like [Helvetica](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/helvetica),[^sn-helvetica] but with more open [apertures](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/aperture) and rounder forms, a deliberate move toward legibility at the tiny sizes of a watch face. Its [Figures](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/figures) and punctuation are tuned for interfaces, with tabular figures for times and numbers that should not jitter.
