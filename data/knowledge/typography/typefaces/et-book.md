# ET Book

> *Bembo, made for a statistician's pages and then given to the web.*

---

## The Face

ET Book is the typeface Edward Tufte[^sn-edward-tufte] used for his books on information design, designed by Dmitry Krasny, Bonnie Scranton, and [Edward Tufte](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/edward-tufte) and based on [Bembo](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/bembo), the revival of [Francesco Griffo](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/francesco-griffo)'s Aldine roman. Tufte released it freely in 2015, and it became the default face of Tufte CSS, a stylesheet for web essays in the manner of his books.

## Character

An [Old-Style](/knowledge/typography/classification/old-style) text face with old-style [Figures](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/figures), a modest [X-Height](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/x-height), and the light, calm texture of Bembo, designed to sit beside small multiples, sparklines, and sidenotes without competing with them.

## In This Vault

ET Book and Tufte CSS shape KJVstudy.org, whose typography is Tufte-inspired, and the sidenote conventions of this vault's essays descend from the same tradition (see [Building for Contemplation When Spiritual Practice Becomes Code](/essays/2025-11-23-building_for_contemplation_when_spiritual_practice_becomes_code) and [The Visual Display of Quantitative Information](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-visual-display-of-quantitative-information)).
