# Gotham

> *A typeface lifted from the side of a bus terminal and given the keys to a city.*

---

## The Face

Gotham was designed by [Tobias Frere-Jones](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/tobias-frere-jones)[^sn-tobias-frere-jones] at Hoefler & Frere-Jones, with Jesse Ragan, and released in 2000 after a commission from GQ magazine. Frere-Jones drew on the plain, sturdy lettering of mid-century New York buildings, notably the signage of the Port Authority Bus Terminal: vernacular letters made by engineers rather than type designers.

## Character

A broad [Geometric Sans](/knowledge/typography/classification/geometric-sans) with wide, round forms and a confident, American plainness, closer to architectural lettering than to the European geometry of [Futura](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/futura). It has a relatively large [X-Height](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/x-height) and generous widths, which keep it legible and friendly.

## Fame

Gotham became the defining face of the 2008 Barack Obama presidential campaign and was carved into the cornerstone of One World Trade Center. It became so common in branding in the following decade that it reads, now, as the sound of earnest, approachable institutions. Its story is also part of the partnership and later dispute between [Jonathan Hoefler](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/jonathan-hoefler) and Frere-Jones.
