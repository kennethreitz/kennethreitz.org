# iA Writer Duo

> *Monospace, loosened just enough to let the m breathe.*

---

## The Face

iA Writer Duo is one of three fonts released in 2018 by Information Architects, the makers of the iA Writer app, alongside iA Writer Mono and iA Writer Quattro. All three are derived from [IBM Plex](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/ibm-plex) Mono under its open license and are open source themselves. iA Writer had long been the model of the distraction-free writing app, and its use of a [Monospace](/knowledge/typography/classification/monospace)[^sn-monospace] face for prose made the typewriter feel of writing part of its identity.

## Duospace

Duo is *duospaced*: most characters keep a fixed width, but a few naturally wide letters, such as **m**, **w**, **M**, and **W**, get one and a half times the cell. It keeps the calm, even rhythm of monospace while avoiding the cramped **m** that makes fixed-width prose tiring. Quattro relaxes the grid further into four widths.

## In This Vault

iA Writer is the direct inspiration for the writing side of the Reitz LaTeX theme, which sets the editor in IBM Plex Mono, the same root these fonts grew from (see [Draft to Document Why I Made My Notes Look Like LaTeX Papers](/essays/2026-11-04-draft_to_document)).
