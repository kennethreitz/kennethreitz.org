# Univers

> *A typeface designed as a system before anyone talked about systems.*

---

## The Face

Univers was designed by the young Swiss designer [Adrian Frutiger](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/adrian-frutiger) and released by the Paris foundry Deberny & Peignot in 1957, the same year as [Helvetica](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/helvetica). Unusually, it was conceived from the start as a complete, coordinated family: twenty-one variants planned together, rather than a regular weight with relatives added later.

## The Numbering

Frutiger replaced vague names like "bold condensed" with a two-digit grid. The tens digit gives the weight (from 3 for the lightest upward, with 5 as regular) and the units digit gives the width and posture, so Univers 55 is the regular roman, 65 is bold, and 57 is regular condensed. The system influenced how type families have been organized ever since, including Neue Helvetica.

## Character

Univers has slightly more [Stroke Contrast](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/stroke-contrast) and more subtle modulation than Helvetica,[^sn-helvetica] with stroke endings cut cleanly. It was designed for both metal and the new [Phototypesetting](/knowledge/typography/history/phototypesetting) machines of Deberny & Peignot's Lumitype, one of the first faces planned with the coming technology in mind. It was a mainstay of the [Swiss International Style](/knowledge/typography/history/swiss-international-style).
