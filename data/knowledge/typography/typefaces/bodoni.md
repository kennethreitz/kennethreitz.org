# Bodoni

> *The type of a man who wanted the page to look like it was engraved in light.*

---

## The Face

Bodoni refers to the types of [Giambattista Bodoni](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/giambattista-bodoni),[^sn-giambattista-bodoni] printer to the Duke of Parma, and to the many revivals of them. Over a long career Bodoni moved from the [Transitional](/knowledge/typography/classification/transitional) style toward the extreme contrast and vertical stress of the [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone), arriving at it in the 1790s in parallel with the [Didots](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/firmin-didot) in Paris.

## Character

Hairline [serifs](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/serif) without brackets, extreme [Stroke Contrast](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/stroke-contrast), ball [terminals](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/terminal), a strictly vertical axis, and a dramatic, sparkling page. Bodoni's books were monuments of whitespace and large type rather than economical reading editions. His types and specimens were collected in the posthumous [Manuale Tipografico](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/manuale-tipografico) (1818).

## Revivals

Twentieth-century revivals include Morris Fuller Benton's Bodoni for American Type Founders (around 1909) and the Bauer Bodoni of 1926, often thought the most faithful. Because the hairlines thin to nothing at small sizes, Bodoni works best in display. It was beloved of mid-century magazine design and remains a fashion staple alongside [Didot](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/didot).
