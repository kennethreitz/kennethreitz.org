# Pairing Typefaces

> *Two typefaces should have a conversation, not an argument and not a monologue.*

---

## The Principle

Most designs need one or two typefaces; a few need three. Pairing them well means finding faces that are different enough to make a clear distinction (see [Typographic Hierarchy](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/typographic-hierarchy))[^sn-typographic-hierarchy] yet share enough to belong together.

## Approaches

| Approach | Example |
|---|---|
| Use one superfamily | [IBM Plex](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/ibm-plex) Sans, Serif, and Mono share structure and proportions |
| Same designer or era | [Hermann Zapf](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/hermann-zapf)'s [Palatino](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/palatino) with his [Optima](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/optima); [Matthew Carter](/knowledge/typography/type-designers/matthew-carter)'s [Georgia](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/georgia) with [Verdana](/knowledge/typography/typefaces/verdana) |
| Shared skeleton | A [Humanist Sans](/knowledge/typography/classification/humanist-sans) with an [Old-Style](/knowledge/typography/classification/old-style) serif, since both descend from the pen |
| Strong contrast | A [Didone](/knowledge/typography/classification/didone) headline over a [Neo-Grotesque](/knowledge/typography/classification/neo-grotesque) text, distinct in every respect |
| Text and code | A serif or sans for prose with a [Monospace](/knowledge/typography/classification/monospace) for code, matched in [X-Height](/knowledge/typography/anatomy/x-height) |

Bringhurst's advice (see [The Elements of Typographic Style](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-elements-of-typographic-style)) is to look to the history and structure of faces, pairing those with shared roots, rather than to surface style alone.

## Practical Checks

Match x-heights at the sizes you will use; check that weights balance on the page; avoid pairing two faces that are similar but not the same, which looks like a mistake; and give each face a clear job.
