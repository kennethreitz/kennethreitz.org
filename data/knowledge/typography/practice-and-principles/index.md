# Practice & Principles

> *Good typography is mostly a long list of small kindnesses to the reader.*

---

How typography is actually done: the principles that govern the page, the line, and the letter. Most of them come down to one idea, that typography exists for the reader (see [The Crystal Goblet](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-crystal-goblet) and [The Elements of Typographic Style](/knowledge/typography/key-texts/the-elements-of-typographic-style)), which is the typographic form of [For Humans](/knowledge/philosophy/for-humans). These notes move from the scale of the page ([Typographic Hierarchy](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/typographic-hierarchy), [Grid Systems](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/grid-systems), [Page Canons](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/page-canons), [Vertical Rhythm](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/vertical-rhythm)), through the line ([Justification and Hyphenation](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/justification-and-hyphenation), [Microtypography](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/microtypography), [Hanging Punctuation](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/hanging-punctuation)), to the letter ([Small Caps](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/small-caps), [Figures](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/figures), [Quotes and Dashes](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/quotes-and-dashes)), and finally to systems and media ([Pairing Typefaces](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/pairing-typefaces), [OpenType Features](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/opentype-features), [Screen Typography and Hinting](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/screen-typography-and-hinting), [Typesetting Math with TeX](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/typesetting-math-with-tex), [Readability and Legibility](/knowledge/typography/practice-and-principles/readability-and-legibility)).
