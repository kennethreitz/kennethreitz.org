# Waxing Gibbous  ·  🌔

> *Almost is its own discipline.*

---

Between the [First Quarter](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/first-quarter) and the [Full Moon](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/full-moon) the Moon swells lopsided and bright — obviously almost full, and for several nights, stubbornly not. This is the gibbous phase, and its territory is *almost*: the project that works but isn't right yet, the skill that performs but hasn't settled into the hands, the loaf that needs ten more minutes precisely when the kitchen smells finished.

The crisis of action is behind; the revelation is ahead. What the gibbous asks for is refinement — analysis, adjustment, the unglamorous passes that separate done from good. It's the editor's phase, the sound-check phase, the code-review phase. Its characteristic temptation is impatience: shipping at ninety percent because ninety percent is visible from here. But the Moon is instructive on this point — it does not skip nights, and the last quarter of light takes as long to arrive as the first.

The counsel of the phase: trust the trajectory, keep adjusting, and let fullness come on its own schedule. It will — that's the one thing the cycle guarantees.
