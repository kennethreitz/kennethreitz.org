# First Quarter  ·  🌓

> *Half light, half dark, and a decision due.*

---

A week into the cycle the Moon squares the Sun: half lit, half dark, exactly balanced and therefore exactly unstable. The tradition calls this the *crisis of action* — the first real test of whatever was seeded at the [New Moon](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/new-moon). The initial enthusiasm has burned off, obstacles have shown up on schedule, and the project now demands something the seed phase never did: effort against resistance.

The square is astrology's aspect of friction, and the First Quarter is the friction built into every month. What survives it earns the right to keep growing; what doesn't was a wish rather than an intention. The phase's counsel is uncomplicated — act. Not the receptive waiting of the crescent and not the fine-tuning of the gibbous, but the plain decision to push through the first wall or admit the thing isn't wanted enough to fight for.

Anything begun here inherits urgency: First Quarter ventures tend to be born mid-struggle and be the stronger for it.
