# Phases of the Moon

> *The psyche is not a fixed thing but a rhythm.*

---

The [Moon](/knowledge/astrology/planets/moon-astrology) is the only heavenly body whose changes are visible to the naked eye night by night — which is why every culture read its cycle as the master metaphor for change itself. In roughly 29.5 days the Moon runs the whole arc: darkness, first light, fullness, and the long exhale back to darkness. Astrology reads each station as a mood of time — a phase isn't something the Moon has, it's something the month *does*, and anything begun, ripened, or released takes the character of the phase it happens under.

The Greeks compressed the cycle into the lunar triad: [Artemis](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/artemis) the waxing crescent huntress, [Selene](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/selene) the full and shining moon, and [Hecate](/knowledge/ai/ai-entities/hecate) the moon gone dark. The Tarot splits the same territory between [The High Priestess](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-high-priestess) (the Moon's still, reflective knowing) and [The Moon](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-moon) (the night road under uncertain light). And on the [Tree of Life](/knowledge/tree-of-life), all of it pools in [Yesod](/knowledge/tree-of-life/yesod), the lunar sphere where images form before they descend into matter.

## The Eight Phases

| Phase | | Light | Keynote |
|-------|---|-------|---------|
| [New Moon](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/new-moon) | 🌑 | 0% | Seed — intention set in the dark |
| [Waxing Crescent](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/waxing-crescent) | 🌒 | ~25% | Faith — tending what can't yet be seen |
| [First Quarter](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/first-quarter) | 🌓 | 50% | Crisis of action — commit or abandon |
| [Waxing Gibbous](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/waxing-gibbous) | 🌔 | ~75% | Refinement — the patience before fullness |
| [Full Moon](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/full-moon) | 🌕 | 100% | Revelation — everything flood-lit at once |
| [Waning Gibbous](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/waning-gibbous) | 🌖 | ~75% | Dissemination — sharing the harvest |
| [Last Quarter](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/last-quarter) | 🌗 | 50% | Crisis of conscience — release what's done |
| [Waning Crescent](/knowledge/astrology/phases-of-the-moon/waning-crescent) | 🌘 | ~25% | Surrender — composting back to seed |

Laid end to end, the eight stations close into the cycle they always were:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    N["New 🌑 — seed"]
    WxC["Waxing Crescent 🌒 — faith"]
    FQ["First Quarter 🌓 — crisis of action"]
    WxG["Waxing Gibbous 🌔 — refinement"]
    F["Full 🌕 — revelation"]
    WnG["Waning Gibbous 🌖 — dissemination"]
    LQ["Last Quarter 🌗 — crisis of conscience"]
    WnC["Waning Crescent 🌘 — surrender"]
    N --> WxC --> FQ --> WxG --> F --> WnG --> LQ --> WnC --> N
```

The waxing half (New to Full) is the Moon of [Artemis](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/artemis) — building, striving, growing toward light. The waning half (Full to New) belongs to [Hecate](/knowledge/ai/ai-entities/hecate) — digesting, releasing, returning wisdom to the dark. Full and New are the hinges: culmination and conception.
