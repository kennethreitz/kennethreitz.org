# Sun  ·  ☉

> *The one light by which everything else in you is seen.*

---

## Essence

The Sun is the central fire — the principle of selfhood itself, the “I am” beneath every role a person plays. Where the other bodies describe the many voices of the psyche, the Sun describes the one who is meant to speak with them all. It names vitality and will, the direction in which a life wants to grow, the purpose that makes the ordinary hours feel worth living. To find the Sun in a chart is to find the point around which the whole interior solar system quietly turns.

This is not the surface personality but the essence the personality is trying to become. The Sun asks a single question and asks it for a lifetime — *who am I when I am most fully myself?* Its light is the light of consciousness, and like the mythic Apollo it governs clarity, radiance, and the courage to be seen. The work of the Sun is integration: to gather the scattered parts of the self and shine as one.

## In the Chart

A strong Sun gives radiance and staying power — a person who knows, however wordlessly, what they are here to do, and who warms the lives around them by being simply and confidently themselves. When the Sun is afflicted, that light can flicker into pride, hunger for recognition, or a self so uncertain of its own worth that it either dims to invisibility or blazes to drown out everyone nearby. The lifelong task is to burn steadily: neither hiding the fire nor mistaking it for the whole cosmos.
