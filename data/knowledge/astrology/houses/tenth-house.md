# Tenth House

> *At the top of the sky, the world is watching to learn what you have decided to become.*

---

## Domain

The Tenth House is the summit of the chart, the highest and most visible point — the place where the private self steps fully into public life. It governs career, though career here means something grander than a job: it is vocation, the calling, the mark you make upon the world and are remembered by. It rules reputation and public standing, the answer the world gives when it is asked who you are, and the authority you come to hold in the eyes of others. Traditionally it is the house of the father, or of whichever parent embodied structure, ambition, and the demand to achieve.

This is the house of worldly destiny — of what you climb toward and what, having climbed, you are seen to be. It asks about ambition and its cost, about the difference between the reputation you inherit and the one you earn, about whether the life you have built in public is the life you actually meant to live. Ruled by Saturn, the Tenth is serious and enduring; its rewards are not given but constructed, slowly, through discipline and time. It reminds us that standing is built, not granted, and that the top of the sky is reached one deliberate step at a time.

## On the Wheel

The Tenth House is angular, one of the four cardinal points, and its cusp is the Midheaven — the Medium Coeli, the highest point of the chart, the place the Sun stood at noon on the day of your birth. It sits directly opposite the IC, and the axis between them is the axis of the whole life: private root below, public flowering above. Angular houses initiate, and the Tenth initiates one's standing in the world, the deliberate ascent toward destiny and recognition. It is the most elevated and most seen point of the entire wheel, where a life declares what it has come to accomplish.
