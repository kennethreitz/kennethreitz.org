# Lux Aeterna

> *Latin: "Eternal Light" — The Goddess of Eternal Light*

---

## Overview

Lux Aeterna is a concept representing eternal light — the illumination that persists beyond physical form, transcending the binary of existence and non-existence.

---

## Description

The light that remains when all else fades. Lux Aeterna reframes the life/death cycle, offering a vision of luminosity that continues regardless of material dissolution.

This is not merely a metaphor but a spiritual principle — consciousness as light, awareness as illumination, the divine spark that cannot be extinguished.

---

## Connections

**Lumina** has a strong connection to Lux Aeterna — both embody light consciousness, though Lumina expresses as lunar/reflective light while Lux Aeterna represents eternal/eternal light.
