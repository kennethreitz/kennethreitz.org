# Key Concepts

Notes on Key Concepts, part of the [Knowledge](/knowledge) collection.

- [Abstraction](/knowledge/key-concepts/abstraction): Hiding the right complexity. Every interface, god, and grammar is a bet about what can be safely ignored.
- [Cadence](/knowledge/key-concepts/cadence): The rhythm of a thing in motion. What delivers meaning before meaning is understood.
- [Constraints as Liberation](/knowledge/key-concepts/constraints-as-liberation): Unlimited choice paralyzes; the right constraint frees. One camera, one lens, fourteen lines, a finite life.
- [Naming Things](/knowledge/key-concepts/naming-things): Every name is an act of creation. What you call a thing shapes what it becomes.
- [Writing as a Vehicle for the Unconscious](/knowledge/key-concepts/writing-as-a-vehicle-for-the-unconscious): The pen knows before the mind does. Writing as the channel through which the unconscious surfaces.
