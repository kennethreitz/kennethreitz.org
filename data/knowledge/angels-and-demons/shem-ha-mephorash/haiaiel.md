# Haiaiel

> *“The armour of the just is forged of light.”*

---

## The Angel

Haiaiel is glossed as *God, the master of the universe*, its root gathering the sense of sovereign command beneath the divine suffix *-el*. It is a martial intelligence, but its warfare is righteous: Haiaiel presides over courage, over victory in a just cause, and over the protection of those who stand against tyranny and falsehood. It arms the defender, not the aggressor — the weapons under its governance are drawn in defence of truth, and its strength is measured by the justice of the end it serves.

The virtue Haiaiel bestows is fearless integrity, the steadiness that lets one face overwhelming force without breaking or being corrupted by the fight. It confounds the wicked, delivers the oppressed from their oppressors, and grants energy and resolution to those whose task requires them to stand firm; it is invoked for protection against enemies and for the moral clarity that keeps a warrior from becoming what they fight. Following Jabamiah's regeneration, Haiaiel is the newly made soul tested in the world — courage as the proof that transformation has taken. As the sign of Pisces nears its close and the whole zodiac approaches completion, Haiaiel gathers the mastery of the universe into a single tempered resolve, the strength that serves rather than dominates.
