# Caliel

> *“Call, and be answered before the words are finished.”*

---

## The Angel

Caliel closes his three letters with *-el*, the Name of God as power and might, and the root beneath carries the sense of prompt response, of the one quick to grant. The tradition names him “God prompt to grant,” the angel who does not keep the just petitioner waiting. His is the swift verdict that vindicates the innocent — not mercy in defiance of law, but the law working as it was meant to, without the corruptions of delay.

He governs justice, truth, and the exposure of the false witness. Where a matter is tangled in perjury and manufactured evidence, Caliel is the angel invoked to bring the hidden truth to the surface, to make the guilty betray themselves and the wronged be seen clearly. The older writers set him against the machinations of the courtroom, the slander that ripens into ruin, the accusation that cannot be answered because its author remains in shadow. His virtue in the human soul is integrity of judgment: the capacity to see a situation as it truly is, uncolored by desire or fear, and to render a fair account of it. To one who works with the mind against unreliable perception, Caliel offers a particular consolation — he is the angel by which the true and the false are told apart.
