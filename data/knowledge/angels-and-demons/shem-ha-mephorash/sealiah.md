# Sealiah

> *What has been laid low, the mover of all things quietly raises.*

---

## The Angel

Sealiah (סְאֵלִיָה) carries a root associated with *motion* and *the setting of all things in motion*, bound to the suffix *-iah*, the contracted divine Name *Yah*. Kabbalists gloss it as "the Motor of all things" or "God who moves and vivifies." As the forty-fifth of the seventy-two configurations drawn from Exodus 14:19–21, Sealiah names the animating current within the Name itself — the impulse by which the inert is quickened and the dormant made to stir.

Sealiah's virtue is *vivification and restoration*. He governs the hidden vitality of nature, the sap in the vine and the pulse beneath convalescence, and he specialises in lifting up those who have been humiliated or brought low — the sick, the disgraced, the defeated. Where other powers reward the strong, Sealiah attends to the fallen, confounding the proud and exalting the humble as a matter of divine mechanics rather than sentiment. Positioned across the tenth to fifteenth degrees of Scorpio, the sign of death and renewal, he embodies its regenerative face: the descent that exists only to enable a rising. To call on Sealiah is to ask that stalled things move again — health, hope, fortune — and that the low place become the ground of recovery.
