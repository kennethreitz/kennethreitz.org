# Lecabel

> *“Knowledge is the light by which the field is tilled.”*

---

## The Angel

Lecabel derives from a root carrying the sense of instruction and comprehension — *lāqaḥ*, “to take, to receive, to learn” — sealed with the suffix *-el*, “God,” and is traditionally translated “God who instructs” or “God who inspires.” It is a name of applied intelligence, of the mind bent not toward abstraction but toward the ordering of the visible world.

Opening the sign of Virgo at its first five degrees, Lecabel presides over the earthy competence proper to that sign: agriculture, calculation, and the illumination of talent. It is the angel of those who resolve difficult problems by measurement and method, who read the ledger and the almanac alike, and who make barren ground yield through knowledge rather than force. The virtue it confers is inspired diligence — a clarity of practical understanding that grants success in enterprises requiring both learning and labour, and a love of the honest work by which the earth is made to give. Under its influence the astronomer, the surveyor, and the farmer share a single gift: the ability to see the hidden order in things and to align themselves with it. Comparative practice sets Lecabel against the Goetic president [Foras](/knowledge/angels-and-demons/ars-goetia/foras), likewise a teacher of logic, ethics, and the virtues of herbs and stones; where [Foras](/knowledge/angels-and-demons/ars-goetia/foras) confers cunning and invisibility, Lecabel confers the honest, luminous knowledge that builds and sustains.
