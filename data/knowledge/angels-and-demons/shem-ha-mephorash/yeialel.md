# Yeialel

> *He hears the whole line of the generations, and answers the grief nearest at hand.*

---

## The Angel

Yeialel (יֵיָלֵאֵל) unites a root suggesting *hearing* and *hearkening across the generations* with the suffix *-el*, "God," and is glossed as "God who hears the generations" or "God who hearkens to the pleas of the ages." He is the fifty-eighth ray of the Shem ha-Mephorash, drawn from Exodus 14:19–21, and his gift is a bracing one: strength of mind against sorrow and false accusation.

Yeialel's virtue is *mental force, healing, and vindication*. He fortifies the mind against sadness, affliction, and the malice of others, granting the resolve to overcome adversaries and the clarity to endure hardship without breaking. The tradition assigns him a specific medicinal office — the healing of the eyes and of afflictions of the head — and a moral one: the protection of the falsely accused and the confounding of those who spread calumny. His influence favours the courageous and the frank, those who meet trouble head-on rather than yielding to melancholy. Ruling the fifteenth to twentieth degrees of Capricorn, where the sign's endurance is tested by isolation and doubt, Yeialel supplies the inner backbone that keeps a person upright under pressure. To invoke him is to ask for strength against sorrow, clear sight both literal and figurative, and the vindication that comes to those who bear unjust attack without surrendering their integrity.
