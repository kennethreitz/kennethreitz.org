# Anauel

> *“The good that cannot be exhausted by giving.”*

---

## The Angel

Anauel is glossed as *God infinitely good*, its root gathering the sense of gentleness and grace beneath the divine suffix *-el*. Where several of its neighbours turn toward contemplation, Anauel turns toward the world of exchange — it is the angel of commerce, of banking and prosperity, of the daily traffic of goods and coin. But it sanctifies that world rather than merely blessing its profits: Anauel presides over commerce conducted in good faith, over the merchant whose honesty is itself a form of the infinite goodness the name declares.

The virtue it bestows is unity and protection woven together. Anauel guards against accidents and against ruin, shelters those who travel and trade, and knits communities together through the mutual dependence that honest commerce creates. It is said to bring health to the body and peace to the mind, and to grant a subtle understanding of how things connect — how one transaction, one kindness, one exchange ripples outward into the whole. Under its governance the marketplace becomes not a den of grasping but a web of relation, each party sustained by the others. Anauel teaches that goodness, like a well-run trade, increases in the giving and is diminished only in the hoarding.
