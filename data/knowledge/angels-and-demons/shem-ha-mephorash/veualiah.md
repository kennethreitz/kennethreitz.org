# Veualiah

> *“The throne that is founded on peace outlasts the throne founded on fear.”*

---

## The Angel

Veualiah is translated “God the King who rules” or “the King and Dominator God,” a name of sovereign authority sealed with the divine suffix *-iah*. It is the angel of just rule and enduring stability, and its office opens the sign of Scorpio at its first five degrees, tempering that sign's intensity toward the steady maintenance of what has been established.

Governing the first pentad of Scorpio, from zero to five degrees, Veualiah presides over peace, prosperity, and the stability of governments and estates. Its classical office is to establish and preserve dominion — to raise up rightful authority, to consolidate what has been won, and to secure the prosperity of kingdoms and households against upheaval. The virtue it bestows is legitimate strength joined to the love of peace: the capacity to hold power without tyranny, to build wealth without ruin, and to endure through the shifts of fortune. Under its influence tottering enterprises are made firm, discord within a realm or a family is quieted, and the foundations of prosperity are laid deep enough to last. It is invoked by rulers and administrators, by those who would establish something meant to endure, and by any who seek stability after a season of turbulence. In the comparative scheme Veualiah answers the Goetic marquis [Sabnock](/knowledge/angels-and-demons/ars-goetia/sabnock), a warlike spirit who builds towers and fortifies them with weapons; where [Sabnock](/knowledge/angels-and-demons/ars-goetia/sabnock) fortifies the stronghold for battle, Veualiah founds the peace that makes the stronghold unnecessary, ruling by stability what the other holds by force.
