# Lelahel

> *“Light does not argue with darkness; it simply arrives.”*

---

## The Angel

Lelahel means *the Praiseworthy God*, or *God worthy of being exalted*, and it closes the sign of Aries in the sixth station, from twenty-five to thirty degrees. The name doubles a root of praise before joining it to the divine *-el*, so that the angel becomes a vessel of radiance — the celebrant of a light that is both physical illumination and the inner clarity of understanding. Of all the Aries angels it is the most solar, governing brilliance, renown, and the arts that flourish under favourable notice.

The tradition assigns Lelahel the power of healing, particularly the healing that restores vitality and lifts the spirit toward joy. It grants success in the sciences and the fine arts, love won by legitimate means, and a fame that is deserved rather than seized. Those it favours are ambitious in the honourable sense, eager to distinguish themselves through genuine merit, and inclined to seek fortune through talent well used. Lelahel stands opposite the Goetic duke [Valefor](/knowledge/angels-and-demons/ars-goetia/valefor), whose disposition toward theft and treachery is here inverted into the pursuit of glory honestly earned — the demon steals reputation, the angel confers it.
