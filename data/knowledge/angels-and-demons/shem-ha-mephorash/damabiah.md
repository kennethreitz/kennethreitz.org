# Damabiah

> *“Wisdom rises where the deep waters gather.”*

---

## The Angel

Damabiah is rendered *God the fountain of wisdom*, and its imagery is aqueous throughout: the root evokes the source, the wellspring, the flowing deep, and the divine suffix *-iah* seals it with the presence of *Yah*. It is the angel of the waters — of the sea and the enterprises undertaken upon it, of rivers and their commerce, of every human endeavour that trusts itself to the current. But the wisdom it dispenses is not merely nautical craft; it is the deeper sense of knowing how to move with a force greater than oneself, how to read the tides of a situation and set one's sail accordingly.

The virtue Damabiah bestows is protective foresight over ventures that risk the deep. It shields travellers by water, favours maritime and commercial undertakings, and is invoked against the schemes of sorcerers and against the loss of fortune in a single catastrophic wave. More inwardly, it grants access to the fountain itself — the sense that wisdom is not a possession to be accumulated but a spring to be returned to, inexhaustible so long as one keeps coming back to drink. Under its Aquarian governance the water-bearer's vessel is not empty but overflowing, and Damabiah teaches the art of channelling that abundance without being drowned by it.
