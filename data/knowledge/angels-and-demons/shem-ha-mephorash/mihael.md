# Mihael

> *Where two are joined in fidelity, the merciful Father makes a third seat at the table.*

---

## The Angel

Mihael (מִיהָאֵל) unites a root of *humility, mercy, and warmth* with the suffix *-el*, "God," producing "the Merciful Father" or "God the merciful and generous father." He is the forty-eighth ray of the Shem ha-Mephorash, drawn like the rest from the three seventy-two-letter verses of Exodus 14:19–21, and he closes the sign of Scorpio on a note of tenderness rather than intensity.

Mihael's virtue is *fertility, friendship, and conjugal love*. He presides over marriage and lasting affection, over the fidelity that keeps a bond alive across years, and over the fruitfulness — of the body and of the shared life — that fidelity makes possible. The tradition credits him with preserving peace between spouses, engendering feelings of trust between friends, and granting presentiments and secret intimations to those who love truly. Set over the twenty-fifth to thirtieth degrees of Scorpio, the sign of union and its deepest bonds, Mihael transmutes Scorpio's possessiveness into loyalty and its passion into devotion. To invoke him is to ask that love be both warm and durable, that a household be fruitful and at peace, and that the affections binding people together partake of the generosity of the Father whose name he carries.
