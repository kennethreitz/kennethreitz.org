# Aim

> *“Three heads, and a firebrand to set cities alight.”*

---

## The Spirit

Aim — also known as Aym or Haborym — is the twenty-third spirit of the Ars Goetia, a great and strong Duke of the infernal orders. He is among the most vividly described of the Goetic spirits, appearing with three heads: the grimoire gives him the head of a serpent, the head of a man (in some manuscripts crowned with two stars), and the head of a cat or calf. He rides upon a viper and carries in his hand a lit firebrand or torch, with which — the tradition ominously notes — he sets cities, castles, and great places on fire.

The flaming brand and the triple countenance make Aim one of the more fearsome figures of the Lesser Key, a spirit whose iconography is saturated with fire and destruction.

## Office

Despite his incendiary aspect, the offices the Goetia assigns to Aim are those of counsel and wit. He is said to make a man witty in all manner of ways, and to give true answers concerning private matters. The burning torch, then, functions less as a literal instruction than as an emblem of his consuming and clarifying nature — a spirit of sharp intelligence figured in the imagery of flame.
