# Vassago

> *Of a good nature, and inclined to reveal what is hidden.*

---

## The Spirit

Vassago is the third spirit of the *Ars Goetia* and holds the rank of prince. He is unusual within the grimoire for the temperate character the tradition assigns him: where many of the seventy-two are described as fierce, deceitful, or reluctant, Vassago is called good-natured, and the same description attaches to him in the older scrying literature. His name appears not only in the *Lesser Key* but in earlier crystallomantic texts, where he was summoned by seers gazing into stones and mirrors, which has given him a lasting association with divination by reflection.

That reputation for benevolence and truthful speech made him a favoured spirit among later practitioners of the visionary arts, who counted him among the more approachable powers of the catalogue.

## Office

The *Goetia* states that Vassago declares things past, present, and to come, and discovers what has been lost or hidden away. His office is essentially oracular and investigative — the recovery of concealed knowledge and misplaced objects — which places him close in function to the spirits of divination, though the grimoire ranks him among its princes rather than its diviner-presidents.
