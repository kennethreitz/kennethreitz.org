# Leraje

> *An archer clad in green, who stirs up battles and festering wounds.*

---

## The Spirit

Leraje — spelled variously Leraie, Loray, or Oray — is the fourteenth spirit of the *Ars Goetia* and one of its marquises. Among the seventy-two he is a martial spirit, and the grimoire pictures him accordingly: a gallant archer dressed in green, bearing a bow and quiver. This clear and singular image, free of the hybrid monstrosity that marks many of his fellows, gives him a memorable place in the catalogue as a huntsman or man-at-arms of the invisible order.

His province is strife and its bodily consequences, and the tradition remembers him as a spirit of war and of the injuries that war brings.

## Office

According to the *Goetia*, Leraje causes battles and contentions, and he makes the wounds of those hurt by arrows to putrefy and fester. His office is therefore wholly given over to conflict — the stirring of quarrels into open fighting and the aggravation of wounds — which places him among the small number of spirits in the catalogue whose function is martial harm rather than knowledge, love, or gain.
