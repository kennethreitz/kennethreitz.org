# Zagan

> *He turns one substance into another as easily as water finds its level.*

---

## The Spirit

Zagan is the sixty-first spirit of the Ars Goetia, ranked a Great King and President — a doubled dignity that places him among the more powerful officers of the catalogue. He is described as appearing at first in the form of a bull adorned with the wings of a gryphon, and afterward, when the conjuration proceeds, in the shape of a man. The bovine form recalls the beast of burden and fertility, while the transformation into a human figure mirrors the alchemical character of his office, in which one nature is coaxed into another.

Of the seventy-two, Zagan is preeminently the spirit of transmutation and reversal. His workings turn on the changing of one thing into its opposite or its refinement, and the tradition treats him as a master of both material and mental alchemy.

## Office

The Goetia records that Zagan makes men witty, and can turn wine into water and water into wine, and blood into wine, and wine into blood; he likewise turns metals into coin after the fashion of their proper metal, transforming the base into the current. Beyond these transmutations he is said to make fools wise — a change of the spirit to match his changes of substance. He is, in sum, the officer of conversion, whether of liquids, metals, or minds.
