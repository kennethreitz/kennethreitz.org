# Philosophy

Notes on Philosophy, part of the [Knowledge](/knowledge) collection.

- [Attention](/knowledge/philosophy/attention): Where your attention goes, everything follows: design, consciousness, money, love.
- [Design for the Worst Day](/knowledge/philosophy/design-for-the-worst-day): If it requires your best self to use, it's not designed for humans.
- [Dualism](/knowledge/philosophy/dualism).
- [Existentialism](/knowledge/philosophy/existentialism): Existence precedes essence - freedom, authenticity, and meaning.
- [For Humans](/knowledge/philosophy/for-humans): The design philosophy: Sensible defaults, human-readable, the 90% case.
- [Hard Problem of Consciousness](/knowledge/philosophy/hard-problem-of-consciousness): You can explain everything the brain does and still not touch why any of it is felt.
- [Idealism](/knowledge/philosophy/idealism).
- [Integrated Information Theory](/knowledge/philosophy/integrated-information-theory): Consciousness is not what a system does, but how tightly its information holds together.
- [Materialism](/knowledge/philosophy/materialism).
- [Panpsychism](/knowledge/philosophy/panpsychism): What if consciousness isn't something brains produce, but something reality is already made of?
- [Phenomenology](/knowledge/philosophy/phenomenology).
- [Philosophy of Mind](/knowledge/philosophy/philosophy-of-mind): How does the felt, thinking inner world relate to the matter that seems to produce it?
- [Stoicism](/knowledge/philosophy/stoicism): *Abstract.
- [Strange Loops](/knowledge/philosophy/strange-loops): The thing that creates itself by referring to itself. All the way down. All the way up.
- [Virtue Inversion](/knowledge/philosophy/virtue-inversion): The algorithm didn't destroy virtue. It flipped it like a coin and showed us the other side.
