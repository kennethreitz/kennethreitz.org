# Vishuddha  ·  Throat

> *What is true in you becomes real only when it is spoken.*

---

## Essence

Vishuddha sits at the throat, the narrow passage where the inner life must pass to become audible in the world. Its name means “especially pure,” for this is the centre of purification — the filter through which raw feeling and half-formed vision are refined into truthful expression. Its element is ether, or space, the subtlest of the five and the medium in which sound itself travels; fittingly, its sense is hearing, and its dimension is communication in the fullest sense: speaking, listening, creating, and the courage to let one’s authentic voice be heard.

The throat is where the private becomes shared. Everything the lower centres have gathered — the security of the root, the feeling of the sacral, the will of the solar plexus, the love of the heart — arrives here to be given form and offered outward. To speak one’s truth is a spiritual act, and a costly one; it requires that the inner and outer be brought into alignment, that what is said match what is felt and known. Where that alignment holds, the voice carries a resonance that mere fluency can never counterfeit.

## Balanced & Blocked

An open Vishuddha feels like being able to say the true thing — clearly, kindly, at the right moment — and to listen as fully as one speaks. Such a person is expressive, honest, resonant, at home in their own voice. When the throat is blocked, the words catch: fear of speaking, a chronic gap between what is felt and what is said, timidity, the swallowed truth that curdles into resentment or lodges as a literal tightness in the neck. When it runs overactive, expression floods past listening — talking over others, dominating, a compulsion to be heard that drowns the very truth it means to serve. The work is alignment: to speak only what is true, and to leave silence enough that others may speak theirs.
