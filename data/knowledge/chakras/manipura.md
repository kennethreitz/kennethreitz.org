# Manipura  ·  Solar Plexus

> *Will is only fire that has learned where to point.*

---

## Essence

Manipura burns at the navel, and its name means “city of jewels” — the radiant power-station of the body. This is the fire centre, the seat of will, agency, and the metabolising heat that turns food into energy and intention into action. Where the sacral wants, the solar plexus acts; it is the transition from feeling to doing, from desire to the deliberate exertion of self upon the world. Personal power lives here — not domination, but the healthy sense of “I can,” the capacity to set a course and hold it against resistance. The gut feeling that precedes reasoned thought is Manipura speaking; the churn of nerves before a great effort is its fire being stoked.

Rightly kindled, this centre gives a person warmth, confidence, and direction — the steady inner sun by which one navigates. It is the ego at its best: not a tyrant but an able captain, translating the deeper currents of root and sacral into purposeful movement through a resisting world.

## Balanced & Blocked

A balanced Manipura feels like quiet self-possession — confident without needing to prove it, disciplined without rigidity, able to assert and to yield as the situation asks. Such a person acts from a warm and steady centre of will. When the fire dims, the result is passivity, low self-worth, chronic indecision, a sense of powerlessness, of being pushed around by circumstance and other people’s wills. When it flares out of control, the same energy becomes domineering, controlling, angry, driven by a compulsion to prove itself — fire that scorches what it meant to warm. The tending is a matter of temperature: enough heat to move, not so much that it consumes.
