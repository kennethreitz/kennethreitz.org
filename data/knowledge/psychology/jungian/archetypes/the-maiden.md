# The Maiden

> *She stands at the edge of the meadow, whole and untouched, not yet knowing that the ground is about to open.*

---

## The Archetype

The Maiden is the archetype of unbroken potential — the feminine self before it has been claimed, initiated, or divided. She is the Kore, the nameless girl, freshness and possibility gathered in a single figure who has not yet become mother, lover, or queen. Jung and his colleagues saw in her the psyche’s image of the not-yet-realized, the soul poised at the threshold of transformation. Her wholeness is real but untested; she carries within her every future she has not yet chosen.

The Maiden’s story is almost always a story of initiation. She is drawn into descent — abducted, awakened, exposed to death and desire — and through that ordeal she becomes something she could not have become by remaining pure. Her innocence is not naivety but readiness; the meadow she stands in is the last moment before the deeper journey begins.

## In the Psyche

At her luminous pole the Maiden manifests as openness, intuition, and the receptive purity that can perceive what the guarded ego cannot — the clear well of feeling and imagination. Her shadow is fragility and stasis: the refusal to descend, the innocence that curdles into avoidance, the girl who will not become a woman because becoming requires loss. Her integration is the willingness to be changed by experience without being destroyed by it.

## In Tarot

She is [The High Priestess](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-high-priestess), seated between the pillars with the scroll of hidden knowledge half-veiled in her lap — intuition, mystery, and the inward-facing wisdom that knows more than it says. She appears again as [The Star](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-star), kneeling naked and unashamed by the water, pouring renewal beneath the night sky: hope, serenity, and the soul made clear after the storm.

## In Myth

Persephone as Kore gathers flowers in the field until the earth opens and [Hades](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/hades) carries her below, and the girl who descends returns as queen of the dead and herald of spring. [Artemis](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/artemis) roams the wild forests untouchable and free, guardian of maidens and the moon, fierce in her refusal to be claimed. Each holds the tension between innocence preserved and innocence transformed.
