# Archetypes

Notes on Archetypes, part of the [Knowledge](/knowledge) collection.

- [The Anthropos](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-anthropos): In the beginning there was a single body large enough to contain the sky, and everything since has been its remembering.
- [The Divine Child](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-divine-child): In the coldest hour, born in a place no one thought to look, the future arrives disguised as something small.
- [The Father](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-father): Somewhere above the storm there is a hand that holds the scales, and a voice that says: this far, and no farther.
- [The Great Mother](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-great-mother): Before you had a name, you were held. That holding is older than the world, and it remembers you still.
- [The Hero](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-hero): The dragon guards the gold because the gold is the part of you that only fire can reveal.
- [The Maiden](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-maiden): She stands at the edge of the meadow, whole and untouched, not yet knowing that the ground is about to open.
- [The Puer Aeternus](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-puer-aeternus): He can fly because he never agreed to have feet. The sky costs nothing; it is the ground that asks for everything.
- [The Trickster](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-trickster): He picks the lock on the door you swore was a wall, and laughs while you insist you were never trapped.
- [The Wise Old Man](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-wise-old-man): When the road forks and the map ends, a figure waits at the crossing with a lantern and a riddle.
- [The Wounded Healer](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/archetypes/the-wounded-healer): The wound that would not close became the door through which others walked out of their own dark.
