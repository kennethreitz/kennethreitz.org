# Depression

> *Sadness is weather. Depression is climate, and the climate forgets the sun was ever real.*

---

## Overview

**Depression** is not ordinary sadness amplified. Grief and disappointment move through a person; depression settles over the whole system and stays. It is a disorder of mood, body, cognition, and motivation at once, a flattening that makes the very machinery of feeling go quiet. People in it often cannot point to a cause, and the absence of a reason is part of the cruelty.

## The Shape of It

The signature is **anhedonia**, the loss of pleasure and interest, the inability to want. Alongside it comes a physical heaviness, a **weight and paralysis** that turns small tasks into impossible ones. Thought slows; concentration and memory degrade in a **cognitive slowing** that feels like wading. **Hopelessness** colors the future as foreclosed. **Sleep and appetite change** in either direction, too much or too little of both. At its worst, depression produces **suicidality**, which is not a character flaw or a bid for attention but a symptom of an illness that lies to the person who has it.

## A Pole, Not Only a Disease

Depression exists as a standalone condition (major depressive disorder), but it is also the **depressive pole** of cyclical illnesses. In [Bipolar Disorder](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/bipolar-disorder) it alternates with [Mania](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/mania), and in Schizoaffective Disorder it arrives entangled with psychotic features. The distinction matters clinically, because the same low mood calls for different treatment depending on what it sits inside of.

## Treatment

For unipolar depression the mainstays are **psychotherapy** and **antidepressants**. In bipolar and schizoaffective contexts, antidepressants alone are risky, they can destabilize mood or trigger a switch into mania, so **mood stabilizers** carry the load instead, sometimes with cautious adjunctive support.
