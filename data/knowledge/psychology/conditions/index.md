# Conditions

Notes on Conditions, part of the [Knowledge](/knowledge) collection.

- [Anxiety](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/anxiety): The threat-detection system running at full power with no threat in the room.
- [Bipolar Disorder](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/bipolar-disorder): A mind tethered between two poles, swinging by the gravity of its own chemistry.
- [Burnout](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/burnout): A server that crashes under load doesn't have a character flaw. It has insufficient resources for the demand.
- [Depression](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/depression): Sadness is weather. Depression is climate, and the climate forgets the sun was ever real.
- [Mania](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/mania): The engine is still running, it's just running so far past the redline that the gauge no longer means anything.
- [Narcissism](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/narcissism): No amount of optimism can change the fact that sometimes, people will take advantage of you, in very unexpected and sinister ways.
- [Psychosis](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/psychosis): A break in shared reality, the mind narrating a world the body alone can see.
- [PTSD](/knowledge/psychology/conditions/ptsd): Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder, when the threat is over but the nervous system never gets the message.
