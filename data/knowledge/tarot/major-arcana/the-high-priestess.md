# The High Priestess  ·  II

> *What she knows, she will not say — she will only wait until you know it too.*

---

## Imagery

A veiled woman sits enthroned between two pillars, one black and lettered B, one white and lettered J — Boaz and Jachin, the twin columns of Solomon’s temple, severity and mercy. Behind her hangs a veil embroidered with pomegranates and palms, screening a body of water that stretches beyond sight. A crescent moon rests at her feet; a solar cross lies on her breast; the horned lunar crown sits on her head. In her lap a scroll marked TORA is half-hidden in her robe. She is stillness itself, a threshold that neither invites nor forbids, only waits.

## Upright

The High Priestess is the guardian of the unconscious, the knowing that arrives without argument. Where [The Magician](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-magician) speaks and acts, she is silent and receptive, holding the mysteries that cannot be forced into daylight. Upright, she counsels patience, intuition, and trust in what you sense but cannot yet prove. She is the inner voice heard only when the outer noise subsides — the invitation to stop striving and let understanding surface on its own.

## Reversed

Reversed, the veil thickens into a wall. Intuition is ignored, drowned out by noise or dismissed as unreasonable; the reversed Priestess can mean secrets kept too long, self-deception, or a disconnection from one’s own depths. It may also warn of information withheld, of surfaces mistaken for the whole, or of a person hiding behind mystery rather than genuinely inhabiting it.

## Archetype

She is the Anima in her lunar aspect, and a face of the Great Mother turned inward — the receptive, intuitive ground from which conscious knowing rises. Jung located true wisdom not in the intellect alone but in dialogue with these deeper waters, the Collective Unconscious she quite literally sits before. To meet the High Priestess is to learn that some truths cannot be seized, only received. She is the gate one must pass to reach the deeper Arcana.

## Mythological Echoes

She is Persephone before the descent, Isis veiled, the Delphic Pythia breathing prophecy from a fissure in the earth. She is every temple guardian, every oracle whose knowing comes sideways through dream and trance. The pomegranates on her veil bind her to the underworld’s queen; the water behind her is the amniotic deep from which all forms are born and to which they return.

## In Practice

When the High Priestess appears, the reading asks you to listen rather than to act. Journalling prompt: what do you already know, quietly, that you have not admitted aloud? Sit with a question tonight and let the answer come in the morning rather than tonight.
