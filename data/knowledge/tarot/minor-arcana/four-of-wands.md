# Four of Wands

> *Four poles, a garland, and the whole village pouring out to say: here, at last, is home.*

---

## Imagery

Four tall wands stand upright, joined at the top by a swag of flowers and fruit that forms a canopy or wedding bower. Beyond it, two figures raise bouquets in celebration, their arms lifted in welcome. A manor and its walls rise in the background beneath a bright, clear sky, and the whole scene is drenched in gold and joy. The garland is the visual heart — abundance made into a gateway. The feeling is festive and secure: a threshold decked for arrival, the warmth of belonging after effort.

## Upright

The Four of Wands is celebration, homecoming, the harvest-feast of the fiery suit. It marks a stable milestone — a wedding, a housewarming, a community gathering, the completion of a foundational stage. The energy that launched and expanded has now produced something worth honouring together. This card speaks of joy shared rather than achievement hoarded, of the moment when private labour becomes public rejoicing. Its invitation is to pause and let yourself be welcomed, to mark the threshold before crossing into whatever comes next.

## Reversed

Reversed, the celebration is muted or delayed. The homecoming feels hollow, the gathering strained, the sense of belonging just out of reach. It can point to transitions that lack closure, to a house that is not yet a home, or to internal joy that has not been allowed to become communal. The shadow is not disaster but a quiet dampening — the garland hung but the heart withheld.

## Archetype

Psychologically this is the temenos, the sacred bounded space — four wands marking a protected ground where the psyche can rest and be affirmed by community. Jung understood the fourfold as a symbol of wholeness, the quaternity that stabilises the wandering spirit. Here the fire of individual ambition is contained within relationship and belonging, echoing the generative warmth of [The Empress](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-empress) and the union celebrated by [The Lovers](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-lovers).

## Mythological Echoes

The card gathers every threshold rite of welcome: the wedding canopy of countless traditions, the harvest festivals of Demeter and the grain-goddesses, the maypole ringed with dancers, the returning hero feasted at the hearth. It is the mythic homecoming — Odysseus at his own door, the prodigal met with rejoicing.

## In Practice

This card asks whether you have let yourself arrive. We are quick to launch the next venture and slow to celebrate the last. Where might you build a threshold of honour rather than rushing past it? Gather your people; mark the ground.
