# Page of Wands

> *A boy holds a living branch and studies it as though it might tell him what he is for.*

---

## Imagery

A young figure in a flame-patterned tunic stands in a barren desert landscape, three small pyramids behind him. He holds a tall, leafing wand and gazes up at it with open curiosity, as if listening to what it has to say. His hat is topped with a red plume that echoes the salamanders on his robe — the mythic creatures of fire. His stance is planted and earnest, more contemplative than active. The composition is bright and dry, all yellows and warm earth. The feeling is eager beginning: enthusiasm not yet spent, a spark newly aware of itself and full of questions.

## Upright

The Page of Wands is the messenger of fire — inspiration in its youngest, most curious form. He brings news of a new passion, a creative impulse worth exploring, an invitation to adventure. His energy is exploratory rather than accomplished; he does not yet know where the fire leads, and that not-knowing is his charm and his freedom. This card speaks of discovery, of the courage to begin without a map, of the restless enthusiasm that says yes before it counts the cost. Its invitation is to honour the beginner's spark and follow it with open, undefended curiosity.

## Reversed

Reversed, the spark scatters or stalls. Enthusiasm flares and dies without follow-through; the Page becomes all excitement and no commitment, chasing every new flame and finishing none. It can mark creative frustration, immaturity, a passion that fizzles, or news that disappoints. The shadow is the perpetual beginner who never lets a fire deepen into warmth — restlessness masquerading as freedom.

## Correspondence

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Element** | Fire. Sub-element: Earth of Fire — the grounding, embodying aspect of the fiery suit. The Page is fire made tangible and curious, its potential taking its first material form. |

## Archetype

This is the puer, the eternal youth in his generative aspect — the divine child who carries the promise of renewal and the danger of never landing. Jung saw the puer as the spirit of new possibility, essential to keeping the psyche young and open, yet needing the counterweight of groundedness to bear fruit. The Page's Earth-of-Fire nature is precisely that counterweight beginning to form, echoing the open-hearted leap of [The Fool](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-fool) as it takes its first curious, embodied step.

## Mythological Echoes

The card carries the salamander of legend, the creature said to live in flame unharmed, and every young hero at the outset of his tale — the boy who pulls the sword, the apprentice who first feels the gift stir in his hands. It echoes the divine child of myth, born to bring light, curiosity itself made into a small bright figure at the edge of a great desert.

## In Practice

When this card appears, something new wants your curiosity. Say yes to the spark before you interrogate it to death. Journal on the passion you have been dismissing as impractical, and let yourself, for a moment, simply be a beginner again.
