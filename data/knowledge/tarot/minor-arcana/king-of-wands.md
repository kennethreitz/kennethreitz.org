# King of Wands

> *The fire has learned to hold its shape; now it can light a whole kingdom without consuming it.*

---

## Imagery

A crowned king sits on a throne adorned with lions and salamanders, holding a tall leafing wand upright before him. His robe blazes with a pattern of salamanders — the fire-creatures — and one live salamander rests near his feet, its tail curling toward its mouth in the shape of the ouroboros, sign of completion. His posture is turned slightly, alert and ready to rise, energy barely contained by the throne. The palette is fiery orange and gold. The feeling is mastered power: a man of vision who has learned to direct his flame outward as leadership rather than let it burn inward as impulse.

## Upright

The King of Wands is fire fully matured into visionary leadership — the entrepreneur, the bold ruler, the one who inspires others toward a distant goal. He holds all the passion of the suit, but now governed by will and directed toward creation. He is charismatic, decisive, and generous with his vision, able to see the whole and rally others to it. Where the Knight charged and the Queen radiated, the King builds and leads. This card speaks of natural authority, entrepreneurial daring, and the honour that comes from wielding power in service of a worthy aim. Its invitation is to lead with fire and integrity both.

## Reversed

Reversed, the fire turns tyrannical or hollow. Authority becomes domineering, the visionary a bully; impatience with others hardens into arrogance, and the leader stops listening. Or the flame guts inward as impulsiveness he never learned to master, grand schemes launched and mismanaged, a king ruling by ego rather than vision. The shadow is power divorced from wisdom — the salamander turned dragon, warmth become scorching, leadership become mere command.

## Correspondence

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Element** | Fire. Sub-element: Fire of Fire — the purest, most concentrated expression of the element. The King is fire fully realised and self-governed, flame that has learned to rule itself and so can rule others. |

## Archetype

This is the mature masculine in its solar, generative aspect — the wise king whose authority serves the realm rather than the ego. Jung distinguished the true King, an image of the integrated [The Self](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/the-self), from the tyrant who is possessed by his own power. The ouroboros salamander at his feet signals wholeness, the fire that circles back and completes itself. He is the fulfilment of the fiery suit's whole journey, the will disciplined into vision, kin to the sovereign authority of [The Emperor](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-emperor) and the radiant completion of [The Sun](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-sun).

## Mythological Echoes

The card carries the solar kings and creator-gods whose fire gives life and order — Ra in his sovereignty, the divine ruler whose flame lights the world without destroying it. It echoes Prometheus grown wise, fire not stolen in haste but stewarded well, and every founder-king whose vision outlived him because he learned to govern his own blaze.

## In Practice

When this card appears, it asks you to lead from your fire without being consumed by it. Where are you called to hold a vision and rally others to it? Journal on the difference, in your own life, between wielding power and being wielded by it.
