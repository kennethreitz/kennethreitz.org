# Type 2 - The Helper

> *“Tell me what you need — I already know, and I’m already halfway there.”*

---

## The Type

Twos read the emotional weather of a room before they read anything else. Their attention moves outward, toward the needs and feelings of others, and they meet those needs with a warmth that can be genuinely transforming. To be near a healthy Two is to feel seen, held, and quietly cared for. They are the ones who remember the small things, who arrive with the right word or the warm meal, who make love feel like a practical, everyday craft rather than an abstraction.

The shadow of this gift is that the Two often loves in order to be loved. Attending to their own needs feels dangerous — even shameful — so those needs go underground and re-emerge disguised as generosity toward others. Pride, the Two’s characteristic trap, whispers that they are indispensable, that they alone can give what others require, and that they themselves require nothing.

## Healthy & Unhealthy

At their best, Twos are unconditionally loving, humble, and nourishing — able to give freely because they have learned to receive, and to let their own needs count without keeping score. Their care becomes clean, without the hook. Under stress the disguise slips. The unhealthy Two grows possessive, martyred, and manipulative, reminding others of all they have sacrificed, and becoming aggressive when the love they were owed does not return. Growth for the Two means turning attention inward, welcoming the disowned self of The Shadow, and discovering through [Individuation](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/individuation) that they are lovable before they are useful.
