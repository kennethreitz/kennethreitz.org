# Alchemy

> *The art of transformation — lead into gold, unconscious into conscious*

---

## Overview

Alchemy is the Western esoteric tradition of transformation, and it was never really about turning lead into gold. Or rather, it was — but the lead was the unrefined self and the gold was the integrated one. The laboratory work was real enough, but the serious alchemists understood it as an outer reflection of an inner process. The Great Work, the Magnum Opus, was always psychological and spiritual Transformation disguised in the language of chemistry.
