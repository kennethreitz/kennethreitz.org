# The Emerald Tablet

> *The shortest, densest instruction manual in Western esotericism.*

The Emerald Tablet — the Tabula Smaragdina — is a brief Hermetic text attributed to [Hermes Trismegistus](/knowledge/alchemy/hermes-trismegistus) that contains the entire alchemical worldview compressed into a few paragraphs. Nobody knows when it was actually written. The earliest known version appears in an Arabic work from the 8th century, claiming to translate a much older text. Whether it's genuinely ancient or a medieval synthesis is irrelevant to its power. The ideas in it predate whatever document first recorded them.

The Tablet has been translated hundreds of times. Isaac Newton translated it. So did Albertus Magnus. Every serious alchemist in the Western tradition considered it foundational — the axioms from which the rest of the art could be derived.

---

## The Core Principles

**"As above, so below; as below, so above."** The Principle of Correspondence. This is the Tablet's most famous line and the foundation of Hermetic thinking. What happens at one scale of reality mirrors every other scale. The movements of planets correspond to movements in the psyche. The behavior of atoms corresponds to the behavior of galaxies. The pattern doesn't just resemble — it *is* the same pattern, expressed through different media.

This isn't mystical hand-waving. It's a structural insight about how reality organizes itself. Fractals work this way. Neural networks work this way. The same power-law distributions appear in city populations, word frequencies, earthquake magnitudes, and internet traffic. The alchemists didn't have the mathematics, but they had the intuition: the universe has habits, and those habits repeat across scales.

**"The father thereof is the Sun, the mother thereof is the Moon."** Solar and lunar — active and receptive, conscious and unconscious, sulphur and mercury. Every manifest thing arises from the interplay of these two principles. Neither alone creates. Creation happens in the tension between them. The [transcendent function](/knowledge/psychology/jungian/the-transcendent-function) in Jungian terms — the third thing that emerges when opposites are held together without collapsing into either one.

**"Separate the earth from the fire, the subtle from the gross, gently and with great ingenuity."** This is [Solve et Coagula](/knowledge/alchemy/solve-et-coagula) stated as instruction. The art requires discrimination — knowing what is essential and what is accretion. But the separation must be done *gently*, with *ingenuity*. Not brute force. Not destruction. The alchemist is a surgeon, not a butcher.

**"It ascends from earth to heaven and descends again to earth, and receives the power of the superiors and the inferiors."** The completed work moves freely between registers. Spirit and matter. Theory and practice. Abstract and concrete. The Philosopher's Stone isn't trapped in either realm — it circulates between them, gathering power from both. This is the laboratory and oratory in a single sentence.
