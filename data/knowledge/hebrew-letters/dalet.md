## Essence

Dalet is the door, and its name also carries *dal*, the poor one — the figure who stands at the threshold with open hands. The sages joined the two readings: the door is the letter of humility, possessing nothing itself, defined entirely by what it lets pass. A door that filled its own frame would be a wall. Dalet teaches the emptiness that makes passage possible, the receptivity that is not weakness but function.

As the number four it is stability and manifestation — the four directions, the four elements, the square foundation on which anything lasting is built. It is a double letter under Venus, bearing the pair of fertility and barrenness: the two states of any womb, any field, any doorway through which life either comes or does not. What gimel carries across the desert, dalet receives and delivers into the world.

On the Tree it is the fourteenth path, the horizontal road joining [Chokmah](/knowledge/tree-of-life/chokmah) to [Binah](/knowledge/tree-of-life/binah) — Wisdom to Understanding, straight across the top of the Tree. It is the passage by which the supernal father's force enters the supernal mother's form, and everything below the Abyss is born through it. Its trump is [The Empress](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-empress), the great mother enthroned in her garden, the gate of life itself. Every birth is a dalet: something infinite consenting to arrive through something small.
