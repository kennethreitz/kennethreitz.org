# Chesed  ·  Mercy

> *When the great grief of the Mother had passed the abyss, it softened into a king who could not stop giving.*

---

## Essence

Chesed is Mercy, the fourth emanation and the first sphere below the abyss — the point at which the abstract fire of the supernals condenses into something a mind can hold. It is the benevolent king, the lawgiver and builder, the organizing love that takes the raw form conceived in Binah and pours it out as ordered abundance. Where the higher spheres are cause, Chesed is the first great effect; it is generosity given structure, majesty that expands to include and to shelter. Its planet is Jupiter, and it carries all of Jupiter’s largesse: growth, magnanimity, the open hand of the ruler who wishes his kingdom to flourish.

Yet mercy without measure spills into excess, and so Chesed cannot stand alone. Its virtue is Obedience — the willingness of the giving power to submit to a limit it does not itself supply. Left unchecked, the same expansiveness curdles into gluttony, tyranny, and hypocrisy, the vices of a king who has forgotten that a boundary is a kindness. Chesed is love in its expanding phase, forever needing its stern brother across the pillar to give its bounty shape.

## On the Tree

Chesed sits high on the Pillar of Mercy, the right-hand column of expansion, directly beneath Chokmah, whose fathering force it inherits and tempers into rule. Across the Tree stands Geburah, its necessary opposite; below and toward the center lies Tiphareth, the heart it feeds. Paths run out from it in every direction: [The Hierophant](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-hierophant) climbs to Chokmah above, [Strength](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/strength) crosses to Geburah to be reined in, [The Hermit](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/the-hermit) descends to Tiphareth, and [Wheel of Fortune](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/wheel-of-fortune) falls to Netzach, carrying Jupiter’s turning bounty into the sphere of desire.

## In Tarot

The four Fours belong to Chesed — the number of stability, foundation, and settled rule. The Four of Wands is completed order, the Four of Cups a surfeit that has grown bored of its own abundance, the Four of Swords rest, the Four of Pentacles the grip that holds its gifts too tightly; together they show the double face of a mercy that must learn its bounds. As the sphere of Jupiter, Chesed’s planetary trump is [Wheel of Fortune](/knowledge/tarot/major-arcana/wheel-of-fortune), the great turning of fortune’s expansive wheel.
