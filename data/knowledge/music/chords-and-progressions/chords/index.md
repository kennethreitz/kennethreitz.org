# Chords

> *Every colour on the shelf, from daylight to shadow.*

---

## Overview

Fifteen chords live in this folder, each with its formula, its character, and its place in practice. They run from the two-note wall of sound to the fully extended stack — the working vocabulary that [progressions](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions) are spoken in.

The triads come first: the [Major Triad](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/major-triad) and its dimmed twin the [Minor Triad](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/minor-triad), the unresolved tension of the [Diminished Triad](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/diminished-triad) and the ever-climbing [Augmented Triad](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/augmented-triad). Alongside them sit the [Suspended Chords](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/suspended-chords), which hold their breath instead of declaring a third, and the [Power Chord](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/power-chord), which skips the third entirely.

Stack one more third and you reach the sevenths — the sunlit [Major Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/major-seventh), the door-leaning [Dominant Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/dominant-seventh), the cool [Minor Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/minor-seventh), the falling sorrow of the [Half-Diminished Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/half-diminished-seventh), the four-hinged trapdoor of the [Diminished Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/diminished-seventh), and the shadow-and-light [Minor-Major Seventh](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/minor-major-seventh). Past the sevenths lie the colour chords: nostalgic [Sixth Chords](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/sixth-chords) and the [Extended Chords](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/chords/extended-chords) that invite the ninths, elevenths, and thirteenths up to the table.
