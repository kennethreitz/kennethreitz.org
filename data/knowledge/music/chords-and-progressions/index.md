# Chords & Progressions

> *Notes stacked into colour, colours strung into motion.*

---

If a [scale](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes) is the palette, a **chord** is a colour mixed from it — three or more notes sounded together, built by stacking thirds — and a **progression** is a sequence of those colours in motion, the harmonic sentence a song speaks. Chords give a moment its mood; progressions give a piece its journey, setting up tension and releasing it through the pull of the cadence. Together with [Scales & Modes](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes) and [Musical Keys](/knowledge/music/musical-keys), they are the working grammar of harmony — the same structures pytheory builds in code.
