# Progressions

> *The routes harmony travels, from three chords to eight.*

---

## Overview

Twelve notes in this folder trace the harmonic sentences songs are built from — each one documented with its Roman numerals, its character, and the repertoire that leans on it. The grammar underneath them all is covered in [Cadences](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/cadences), the ways a phrase ends or pretends it won't.

The essentials come first: [I-IV-V](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/i-iv-v), three chords and the truth; [ii-V-I](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/ii-v-i), the sentence every jazz musician speaks fluently; and the [Twelve-Bar Blues](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/twelve-bar-blues), twelve bars wide and deep enough to fall into. Pop and rock run on [I-V-vi-IV](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/i-v-vi-iv) and its dark-starting rotation [vi-IV-I-V](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/vi-iv-i-v), on the slow-dance [Fifties Progression](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/fifties-progression), and on the bittersweet [Royal Road Progression](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/royal-road-progression) that runs straight through Tokyo.

The deeper cuts follow the pull of falling fifths and borrowed colour: the [Circle of Fifths Progression](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/circle-of-fifths-progression), the descending [Andalusian Cadence](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/andalusian-cadence), the eight immortal chords of [Pachelbel's Canon](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/pachelbels-canon), and the [Backdoor Progression](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions/progressions/backdoor-progression) that resolves home through an entrance you didn't expect.
