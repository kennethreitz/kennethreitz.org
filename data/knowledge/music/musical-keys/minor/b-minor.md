# B Minor

> *This is, as it were, the key of patience, of calm awaiting one’s fate and of submission to divine dispensation.*

---

## Character

Christian Schubart’s characterization, from *Ideen zu einer Ästhetik der Tonkunst* (1806), translated by Rita Steblin:

> This is, as it were, the key of patience, of calm awaiting one’s fate and of submission to divine dispensation.
