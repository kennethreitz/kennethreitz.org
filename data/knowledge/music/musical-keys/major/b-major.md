# B Major

> *Strongly coloured, announcing wild passions, composed from the most glaring colours.*

---

## Character

Christian Schubart’s characterization, from *Ideen zu einer Ästhetik der Tonkunst* (1806), translated by Rita Steblin:

> Strongly coloured, announcing wild passions, composed from the most glaring colours. Anger, rage, jealousy, fury, despair and every burden of the heart lies in its sphere.
