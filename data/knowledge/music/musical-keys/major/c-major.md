# C Major

> *Completely pure.*

---

## Character

Christian Schubart’s characterization, from *Ideen zu einer Ästhetik der Tonkunst* (1806), translated by Rita Steblin:

> Completely pure. Its character is: innocence, simplicity, naïvety, children’s talk.
