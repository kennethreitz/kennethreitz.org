# D Major

> *The key of triumph, of Hallelujahs, of war-cries, of victory-rejoicing.*

---

## Character

Christian Schubart’s characterization, from *Ideen zu einer Ästhetik der Tonkunst* (1806), translated by Rita Steblin:

> The key of triumph, of Hallelujahs, of war-cries, of victory-rejoicing. Thus, the inviting symphonies, the marches, holiday songs and heaven-rejoicing choruses are set in this key.
