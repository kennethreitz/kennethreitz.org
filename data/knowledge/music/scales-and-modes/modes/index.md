# Modes

> *One scale, seven centres of gravity.*

---

## Overview

The seven diatonic modes — the rotations of the major scale, each starting on a different degree and shifting the centre of gravity while keeping the same notes. Each note in this folder gives the mode's interval formula, its characteristic tone, and where it turns up in practice.

Read brightest to darkest: [Lydian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/lydian) hovers an inch off the ground with its raised fourth; [Ionian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/ionian) is the major scale itself, the white light before the prism; [Mixolydian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/mixolydian) is major with the top button undone. Then the minor-leaning half: [Dorian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/dorian) keeps one window open to the light through its natural sixth, [Aeolian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/aeolian) is the natural minor telling the plain truth of sadness, [Phrygian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/phrygian) opens a door onto something older and darker, and [Locrian](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes/modes/locrian) — the house built without a foundation — flattens both the second and the fifth.
