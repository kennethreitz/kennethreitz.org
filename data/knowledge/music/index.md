# Music

> *Twenty-plus years of it — the craft beneath the rest of the work.*

---

Music is the oldest of Kenneth's disciplines and the one the others borrow their vocabulary from — cadence, structure, patience. This domain is the reference side of that practice: the technical and expressive building blocks, kept the way the [Tarot](/knowledge/tarot) and [Astrology](/knowledge/astrology) collections are kept. The lived side — the bands, the kit, the releases — lives in Music Background.

---

## Collections

**[Drum Rudiments](/knowledge/music/drum-rudiments)** — the forty essential patterns of the snare, the alphabet of stickings from the single-stroke roll to the triple ratamacue. The vocabulary of every fill and groove.

**[Musical Keys](/knowledge/music/musical-keys)** — the twenty-four keys and their affective characteristics, drawn from Schubart's 1806 catalogue of what each key was once understood to *mean*, back when unequal temperament gave every key its own colour.

**[Scales & Modes](/knowledge/music/scales-and-modes)** — the seven diatonic modes and the core scales beyond them, the raw material of melody and the tonal worlds a piece can live in.

**[Chords & Progressions](/knowledge/music/chords-and-progressions)** — notes stacked into chords and strung into progressions: the working grammar of harmony, from the three-chord backbone to the jazz cadence.
