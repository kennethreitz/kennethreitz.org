# Kun

> *Three broken lines, three open doors — the ground that takes everything in and asks for nothing.*

---

Kun is the pure yin trigram: three broken lines, ☷, each with its gap at the center, offering no resistance anywhere. It is **Earth**, the receptive ground that receives the sky's giving and turns it into form — soil, body, matter, the substance in which every seed must be planted before it can become anything. Where [Qian](/knowledge/trigrams/qian) initiates, Kun completes; where Qian is the will that decides, Kun is the patience that carries and brings to term. This is not weakness but a different strength: the yielding that outlasts the rigid, the softness that water uses to carve stone.

Its action is receptivity, devotion, nourishing support — the capacity to hold and sustain. In the family of the eight, Kun is the **Mother**, the field into which the Father's energy descends and from which the six children are born. Among the [elements](/knowledge/elements) it maps cleanly onto Earth, cold and dark and fertile.

In the **Earlier Heaven** arrangement Kun sits at the bottom, at the north, directly opposite [Qian](/knowledge/trigrams/qian) above it — the two poles between which all the other trigrams are strung. In the **Later Heaven** arrangement it takes the **Southwest**, the place of nourishment and the receptive mother-ground. In divination Kun counsels yielding rather than forcing, service rather than command, the wisdom of the one who supports and endures; its shadow is passivity that never acts at all, the ground that swallows what it was meant to grow.
