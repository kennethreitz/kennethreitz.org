# Vishnu

**Domain**: Preserver, sustainer, protector of the universe
**Symbol**: Conch, discus, lotus, mace, blue skin
**Consort**: Lakshmi (goddess of wealth and prosperity)

---

## Overview

Vishnu is the preserver god in the Hindu Trimurti. He maintains cosmic order (dharma) and protects the universe. When dharma is threatened, he descends to earth in various avatars to restore balance. One of the most widely worshipped deities in Hinduism.

His blue or dark skin represents the infinite, like sky and ocean. Four arms hold the conch (Shankha, sound of creation), discus (Sudarshana Chakra, weapon against evil), lotus (Padma, spiritual liberation), and mace (Gada, power and authority). His vehicle is Garuda, the divine eagle. In his resting form, he reclines on Shesha the cosmic serpent, floating on the cosmic ocean with Lakshmi at his feet, dreaming the universe into being between cycles.

---

## The Ten Avatars (Dashavatara)

"Whenever dharma declines and adharma rises, I manifest myself." -- Bhagavad Gita

1. **Matsya** (Fish) -- Saved humanity from the great flood, rescued the Vedas
2. **Kurma** (Tortoise) -- Supported Mount Mandara during the churning of the ocean
3. **Varaha** (Boar) -- Rescued earth from the demon Hiranyaksha
4. **Narasimha** (Man-Lion) -- Killed the demon Hiranyakashipu, protected devotee Prahlad
5. **Vamana** (Dwarf) -- Subdued demon king Bali; three steps covered the universe
6. **Parashurama** (Rama with Axe) -- Warrior avatar who destroyed corrupt kshatriyas
7. **Rama** (Prince of Ayodhya) -- Hero of the Ramayana, ideal king, embodiment of dharma
8. **[Krishna](/knowledge/mythology/hindu-mythology/krishna)** (The Divine Cowherd) -- Hero of the Mahabharata, teacher of the Bhagavad Gita, most popular avatar
9. **Buddha** (The Enlightened One) -- Gautama Buddha in some traditions; compassion and non-violence
10. **Kalki** (Future Avatar) -- Yet to come, will appear at the end of Kali Yuga riding a white horse

## Major Myths

**Churning of the Ocean (Samudra Manthan)**: Gods and demons churn the cosmic ocean to retrieve the nectar of immortality. Vishnu appears as Kurma to support the mountain and as Mohini (a beautiful woman) to distribute the nectar.

**Cosmic Sleep**: Between cosmic cycles, Vishnu rests on Shesha, dreaming the universe. Brahma emerges from a lotus growing from his navel to begin creation anew.

**Marriage to Lakshmi**: Lakshmi arose from the churning ocean and chose Vishnu as her consort. She incarnates alongside each of his avatars.

## Vaishnavism

The largest Hindu tradition, with Vishnu as supreme deity. Devotion (bhakti) is emphasized, often directed through his avatars -- Krishna and Rama being the most popular. Sacred texts include the Vishnu Purana, Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, and Bhagavata Purana.

## Philosophical Significance

Vishnu pervades all existence ("All this is Vishnu"), maintaining cosmic balance through active engagement with the world. His avatars represent divine immanence: God taking form, becoming accessible, establishing a personal relationship with devotees.

## Archetypal Significance

Divine order and structure, the sustaining principle, the savior archetype through his avatars. Psychologically: maintaining order in the psyche, restoring balance, conscious intervention, integration of the divine and human.
