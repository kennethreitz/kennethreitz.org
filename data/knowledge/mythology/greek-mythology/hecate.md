# Hecate

**Domain**: Witchcraft, crossroads, the moon, ghosts, the liminal
**Symbol**: Torches, keys, the three-way crossroads, black dogs, the serpent
**Roman Equivalent**: Trivia ("of the three ways")

---

## Overview

Hecate is the goddess of witchcraft and the threshold — the power that lives wherever one road becomes three. Daughter of the Titans Perses and Asteria, she predates the Olympians and was allowed by [Zeus](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/zeus) to keep her ancient honors across earth, sea, and sky. She is often shown triple-formed, three bodies facing three directions at once, holding torches to light what is otherwise hidden. She belongs to the places between places: doorways, boundaries, the dark of the moon, the moment before a choice. To pray to her was to ask for safe passage across a line.

She is not sinister so much as unbound. She keeps company with ghosts and hounds, walks the night, and answers to no one — the tutelary spirit of anyone standing at a crossroads unsure which way to turn.

---

## Key Myths

**The witness at the abduction.** When [Hades](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/hades) carried Persephone below, Hecate alone heard the girl's cry from her cave. She came to [Demeter](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/demeter) with her torches and joined the search, and afterward became Persephone's attendant and companion in the underworld — the one goddess who moves freely between the world above and the world below.

**Keeper of the keys.** Hecate holds the keys to the gates between realms. As a goddess of the crossroads she is invoked at boundaries and turnings, and offerings — "Hecate's suppers" — were left for her at three-way junctions on the night of the new moon, food set out in the dark for the goddess and her dogs.

**The moon's dark face.** Where [Selene](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/selene) is the full and shining moon and [Artemis](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/artemis) the crescent huntress, Hecate is the moon gone dark — the third face of the lunar triad, presiding over magic, the underworld, and the hour when the world is least visible and most porous.
