# Uranus

**Domain**: The sky, the heavens
**Symbol**: The starry sky, the night vault
**Roman Equivalent**: Caelus

---

## Overview

Uranus is the sky — the primordial heaven, born of [Gaia](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/gaia) (the Earth) and then her consort, the two of them the first parents of the cosmos. With Gaia he fathered the twelve Titans, the three Cyclopes, and the three hundred-handed Hecatoncheires. But Uranus feared his own monstrous children and pushed them back into Gaia's depths, refusing to let them be born — the first act of a father trying to stop the future from arriving.

He is less a character than a position: the sky as the first ceiling, the first authority, and the first to be overthrown. From his fall the rest of the genealogy unspools.

---

## Key Myths

**The overthrow.** Gaia, in agony from the children trapped inside her, made a great sickle and asked her sons to act against their father. [Cronus](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/cronus) alone agreed; he ambushed Uranus and castrated him, severing sky from earth and ending his reign. The blood that fell birthed the Furies and the Giants; from the foam where his severed flesh met the sea rose [Aphrodite](/knowledge/mythology/greek-mythology/aphrodite).

**The prophecy.** As he fell, Uranus named his children *Titans* ("the strainers") and foretold that they too would be punished for their overreach — the first of the cosmogony's recurring prophecies that a father will be undone by his own son.
