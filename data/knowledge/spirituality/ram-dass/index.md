# Ram Dass

> *Be Here Now — The primary spiritual teacher*

---

## Overview

Ram Dass (1931-2019), born Richard Alpert, is Kenneth's primary spiritual influence. Harvard psychology professor turned mystic, he bridged Eastern wisdom and Western minds, modeling the very transformation Kenneth has undergone — from academic/technical achievement to consciousness exploration.

---

## The Journey

His life falls into three acts, each with its own page. The [psychedelic years](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/the-psychedelic-years) cover Richard Alpert the Harvard psychologist, the psilocybin research with Timothy Leary, and the firing that ended his academic life. In 1967 he traveled to India, met the guru [Neem Karoli Baba](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/neem-karoli-baba) (Maharaj-ji), received the name Ram Dass — "servant of God" — and returned transformed. The teaching years that followed opened with [Be Here Now](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/be-here-now) (1971) and ran until his death on December 22, 2019, deepening late in life into [Loving Awareness](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/loving-awareness) after his 1997 stroke and into [Walking Each Other Home](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/walking-each-other-home), his long meditation on conscious aging and death.

---

## The Teachings

The collection breaks his message into its major currents. [Be Here Now](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/be-here-now) is the foundational instruction — past is memory, future is projection, only this moment is real. [Loving Awareness](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/loving-awareness) is the mature distillation: "I am loving awareness," consciousness recognizing itself through love, being love rather than doing it. [Neem Karoli Baba](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/neem-karoli-baba) holds the guru relationship — the mirror of unconditional love, and the truth that "the guru is within you." [Walking Each Other Home](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass/walking-each-other-home) carries the teaching on death and dying: from somebody to nobody to everybody, ego dissolution as expansion rather than destruction, death as transition. Running underneath all of it is service as practice — [Karma](/knowledge/spirituality/karma) [Yoga](/knowledge/spirituality/yoga), action without attachment — and the witness consciousness that watches experience without being swept away.

## Key Concepts

### Witness Consciousness

The part of you that watches experience without being swept away by it. Ram Dass taught accessing this witness through meditation, mantra, and service.

### [Karma Yoga](/knowledge/spirituality/yoga/four-yogas/karma-yoga)

Service as spiritual practice. Action without attachment to results. Doing what needs doing because it needs doing.

### The Game

Life as divine play (lila). Taking it seriously and lightly simultaneously. "The game is not about becoming anybody. It's about becoming nobody."

### Suffering and Grace

"Suffering is the sandpaper of our incarnation. It does its job of smoothing us out." The wound as teacher.
