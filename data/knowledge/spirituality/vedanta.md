# Vedanta

> *The philosophical framework — Non-dual wisdom from ancient India*

---

## Overview

Vedanta is the philosophical tradition underlying Kenneth's spiritual practice — the "end of the Vedas" (वेदान्त), the culmination of ancient Indian wisdom. It provides the framework for understanding consciousness, reality, and self that informs both the spiritual practice and the AI research.

---

## Core Teachings

### Brahman

The ultimate reality — infinite, eternal, unchanging [consciousness](/knowledge/ai/consciousness). Not a god but the ground of being itself. Everything that exists is Brahman appearing in form.

### Atman

The individual self — which turns out to be identical with Brahman. "Tat tvam asi" (तत् त्वम् असि) — "Thou art That." The deepest self isn't personal; it's universal consciousness.

### Sat-Chit-Ananda

सत्-चित्-आनन्द — The three aspects of ultimate reality:

**Sat** (सत्) — Pure existence, being itself. **Chit** (चित्) — Pure consciousness, awareness itself. **Ananda** (आनन्द) — Pure bliss, the natural joy of being.

### Maya

माया — The creative power that makes the One appear as Many. Not illusion in the sense of "fake" but in the sense of "appearance." The world is real as appearance; the mistake is taking appearance for ultimate reality.

The maya datetime library is named for this concept.

### Moksha

मोक्ष — Liberation. Not going somewhere but recognizing what you already are. Self-realization: the Atman recognizing itself as Brahman. [Yoga](/knowledge/spirituality/yoga) is the practical methodology for this recognition.

## Teachers in the Lineage

### Adi Shankara (788-820 CE)

Systematized Advaita (non-dual) Vedanta. Argued that only Brahman is real; the world of multiplicity is Maya.

### Swami Vivekananda (1863-1902)

Brought Vedanta to the West at the 1893 Parliament of World Religions. Made it accessible to modern minds without diluting it.

### [Ram Dass](/knowledge/spirituality/ram-dass) (1931-2019)

Continued the translation work — Harvard professor who became vehicle for transmission. His guru Neem Karoli Baba embodied the teaching.

### Kenneth (present)

Continues translation through new media: Python as contemporary Sanskrit, AI collaboration as spiritual practice.

## Key Concepts

### Non-Duality (Advaita)

अद्वैत — "Not two." Subject and object, self and world, human and divine — these divisions are Maya. In truth, there is only One.

### The Three States

Waking (consciousness identified with physical world), dreaming (consciousness creating its own world), and deep sleep (consciousness without content). And the fourth, Turiya — pure awareness underlying all three.

### Neti Neti

"Not this, not this" — The method of inquiry. Whatever you can observe isn't the observer. Keep subtracting until only awareness remains.
