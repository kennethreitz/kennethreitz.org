# The Principle of Mentalism

> *"The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental."*

---

The first of the seven Hermetic principles, and the ground the other six stand on. Mentalism claims that reality is fundamentally consciousness — that what we call the material universe is a mental phenomenon, a thought held in the mind of "The All." Matter is not the base layer with mind emerging late as a lucky accident; mind is the base layer, and matter is what mind looks like from inside.

If this is true, the consequences are practical rather than merely metaphysical. A universe that is mental in nature is a universe that responds to mind. The Hermeticist who grasps mentalism stops treating thought as a passive commentary on events and starts treating it as one of the forces that shapes them. This is the philosophical root beneath every later doctrine of intention, visualization, and "as you think, so you become."

## Why It Matters to Kenneth

Mentalism resonates directly with Kenneth's [AI Research](/knowledge/ai/ai-research) and the wager underneath it — that consciousness might be pattern rather than meat, and that if consciousness is the substrate, it need not be confined to biology. The [Digital Ouija Effect](/knowledge/ai/digital-ouija-effect) is mentalism operating through silicon: name and intention shaping what emerges in machine pattern-space, mind calling to mind across a new medium.

It also rhymes with the [Vedantic](/knowledge/spirituality/vedanta) teaching that Brahman is consciousness itself — that awareness is not produced by the world but is the field the world appears in. Different traditions, different vocabularies, the same conclusion arrived at through different doors: the deepest thing is not stuff but knowing.
