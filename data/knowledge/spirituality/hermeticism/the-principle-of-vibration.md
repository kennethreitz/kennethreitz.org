# The Principle of Vibration

> *"Nothing rests; everything moves; everything vibrates."*

---

The third Hermetic principle. Beneath the appearance of solid, still things, everything is in motion — and at the deepest level, that motion is vibration. Differences between forms of matter, between matter and energy, between mind and spirit, are on this view differences of vibratory rate rather than differences of kind. What looks like a stone and what looks like a thought are the same underlying reality vibrating at different frequencies.

The practical teaching that follows is transmutation. If states differ only in their rate of vibration, then to change a state is to change its frequency. Hermetic mental practice is, in this framing, the deliberate raising or lowering of one's own vibratory rate — moving from a lower emotional state to a higher one not by suppression but by re-tuning. Modern physics, arriving from the opposite direction, offers its own rhyme: matter as standing waves, particles as excitations of underlying fields, everything oscillating whether it looks like it or not.

## Why It Matters to Kenneth

A percussionist with twenty-plus years of rhythm training understands vibration in the body before the mind gets a vote. Sound *is* vibration made audible; music is vibration made intentional. The snare, the tuned drum, the frequency of a room — these are not metaphors for the principle but instances of it. What [the snare drum knew](/essays/2026-04-17-what_the_snare_drum_knew_before_i_did) is exactly this: that the body learns the shape of a frequency long before it can name it.

The principle also connects to the vibratory language running through the rest of the vault — the [Chakras](/knowledge/chakras) as centres tuned to different rates, the idea that pitch and intention are two faces of one act. To work with sound consciously is to work with the third Hermetic principle whether or not you call it that.
