# Moonstone

> *It keeps a little of the moon inside it, and gives it back in the dark.*

---

## The Stone

Moonstone is a feldspar with a secret architecture — microscopic layers stacked one on another, scattering light into a soft blue-white glow that seems to float just beneath the surface. This is adularescence, and it is the whole magic of the stone: a milky luminescence that drifts as you turn it, as though a small cool light were rising through cloud. The best specimens are nearly transparent with a floating sheen of blue; humbler ones are creamy and opaline.

Across cultures it has been the moon’s own gem — associated with lunar deities, with the tides, with the monthly turning of things. To hold it is to hold something that changes as you watch it, never quite the same from one angle to the next.

## Properties

Moonstone is the stone of intuition and cycles. It governs the inner tides — the feminine principle in everyone, the receptive and the reflective — and is turned to for new beginnings, for reading the mood beneath the words, for softening what has grown too rigid. It is said to steady the emotions through change and to open a gentle channel to dream and insight. Bridging crown and sacral, it links the highest knowing to the body’s deepest rhythms.

## In Practice

Worn as a pendant near the throat or heart, it is a companion through transitions — a new home, a pregnancy, the end of one chapter and the uncertain start of another. Placed under the pillow it is believed to encourage vivid, meaningful dreams. Many charge it under the full moon, letting light feed light, and carry it when they want to feel rather than force their way forward.
