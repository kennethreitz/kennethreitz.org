# Clear Quartz

> *The empty vessel holds the most.*

---

## The Stone

Clear Quartz is the archetype of what most people picture when they hear the word crystal — a colourless, glass-clear point that catches light and throws it back in rainbows. It grows in long hexagonal prisms terminating in six-sided pyramids, sometimes flawless and sometimes veiled with milky phantoms and fractures that only deepen its beauty. It is one of the most abundant minerals on Earth, and yet each specimen feels singular, as though the light passing through it had been briefly asked to remember something.

To the hand it is cool and certain. There is a reason ancient cultures across the world — from the Greeks who named it *krystallos*, believing it to be eternally frozen ice, to the peoples who carved it into skulls and spheres — treated it as a substance halfway between matter and spirit.

## Properties

Clear Quartz is called the master healer, and the title is earned less through any single specialty than through its extraordinary versatility. It amplifies. Whatever intention, energy, or thought is brought to it, the stone takes up and magnifies, which makes it the great collaborator of the mineral kingdom — pair it with another stone and it strengthens that stone’s work. It clarifies the mind, tunes the whole [chakra](/knowledge/chakras) column, and is prized for holding and broadcasting a programmed intention with remarkable fidelity.

## In Practice

Because it works with everything, Clear Quartz is the stone to reach for when nothing more specific suggests itself. It is held in meditation to focus a single clear thought, placed in a grid to bind other stones into one purpose, or kept at the desk as an amplifier of will. Cleanse it often — under running water, in moonlight, or with breath — since it takes on and holds whatever it is given, for better or worse.
