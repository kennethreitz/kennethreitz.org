# Malachite

> *The heart must break its old shape to grow a truer one.*

---

## The Stone

Malachite is green made hypnotic — a copper carbonate banded in concentric rings and swirling eyes of every shade from pale sea-foam to a green so dark it is nearly black. Cut and polished, its surface reads like a topographic map or the grain of some impossible wood, no two sections alike. The ancients ground it for pigment and for the eye-paint of Egypt, and carved it into amulets against the evil eye, drawn to a colour that seems to hold the vitality of growing things.

It is a soft and heavy stone, cool and dense in the hand, and it carries an intensity that experienced practitioners treat with respect. Malachite is not a gentle companion; it is a powerful one, and the two are not the same.

## Properties

Malachite is a stone of transformation and deep [heart](/knowledge/chakras/anahata)-healing, and it does not do its work by halves. It draws buried emotion — old grief, trauma, patterns long hidden from the self — up to the surface to be released, and it protects fiercely while that excavation runs its course. It is said to absorb negativity and pollution readily, standing guard over the heart as it opens. Because its action is so strong, it is a stone for the ready and the willing rather than the merely curious.

## In Practice

Malachite is placed over the heart in focused sessions to surface and release what has been held there, and worn or carried for protection through demanding passages. As a raw stone it contains copper and can be toxic in powder or water, so it is cleansed dry — with Selenite or in moonlight, never salt water — and handled polished rather than raw when worn against the skin.
