# Aventurine

> *Luck is a green door, and this is the hand that opens it.*

---

## The Stone

Aventurine is a green quartz shot through with tiny platelets of mica or fuchsite that give it a soft, sparkling glimmer — a shimmer called aventurescence, the very effect that lent the stone its name (from the Italian *a ventura*, “by chance”). The green ranges from pale mint to a deep forest, and the internal glitter is gentle, more a warmth than a flash, as though sunlight were caught in leaves.

It is an easygoing stone, common and generous, without the drama of the rarer gems. That plainness suits its character — it feels like a friend who quietly wishes you well, and the colour alone is calming, the green of growing things.

## Properties

Aventurine is the stone of luck and opportunity — traditionally the luckiest of all the crystals, a magnet for chance, growth and new possibility. But its good fortune is the grounded, earthy kind: it works less by conjuring windfalls than by opening the wearer to opportunity and steadying the nerve to seize it. Sitting at the heart, it soothes agitation and quiets the fear of taking a risk, blending optimism with calm. It is turned to for prosperity, for fresh starts, and for the emotional equanimity that lets good things in.

## In Practice

It is a classic pocket stone for anyone hoping to court a little luck — carried into an interview, a negotiation, a game of chance, or the first day of a new venture. Held at the heart in meditation, it eases anxiety and clears space for hope. Gardeners have long tucked it among plants to encourage growth, honouring its old link to green and living things.
