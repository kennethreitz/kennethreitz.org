# Onyx

> *Strength is mostly the quiet decision to stay.*

---

## The Stone

Onyx is a banded chalcedony, a kind of microcrystalline quartz laid down in straight parallel layers. The classic stone alternates black and white bands, and the solid black onyx most people know is usually that same material evened out to a single deep colour. It takes a high, glassy polish, which is why it has been carved into cameos, seals, and beads since the ancient world. The name comes from the Greek *onyx*, meaning fingernail, after a myth that the stone grew from a clipping of Aphrodite's nail.

Onyx also appears in scripture. It is one of the stones set in the high priest's breastplate in Exodus 28, and two onyx stones on his shoulders were engraved with the names of the tribes of Israel, so he carried them before the LORD as a memorial.

## Properties

Onyx is a stone of strength, steadiness, and self-mastery. Where [Black Tourmaline](/knowledge/crystals-and-gemstones/black-tourmaline)[^sn-black-tourmaline] guards a boundary, onyx works on the person holding it, gathering scattered energy back into the body and helping someone endure a hard season without coming apart. It is linked with discipline, grief worked through rather than avoided, and the Saturnine virtue of keeping going. Like most black stones, it is also read as protective, absorbing heavy or hostile energy.

## In Practice

Onyx is worn as a ring or pendant for steadiness through long or difficult stretches, held during grounding meditation, and kept on a desk as a reminder of commitment to hard work. It pairs well with [Hematite](/knowledge/crystals-and-gemstones/hematite) for grounding and [Smoky Quartz](/knowledge/crystals-and-gemstones/smoky-quartz)[^sn-smoky-quartz] for releasing what has been absorbed. It can be cleansed under running water or with smoke.
