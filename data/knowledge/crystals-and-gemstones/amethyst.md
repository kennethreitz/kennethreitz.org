# Amethyst

> *Clear the wine from the mind and the vision arrives.*

---

## The Stone

Amethyst is quartz that has been touched by violet — a colour born of iron and natural radiation working slowly in the dark, ranging from the palest lilac to a deep, ecclesiastical purple. It lines the hollow interiors of geodes in glittering banks of crystal points, and few sights in the mineral world are as arresting as a cave-like amethyst cathedral split open to the light. Tumbled or faceted, it holds its regal colour with a quiet steadiness.

The Greeks gave it its name and its legend: *amethystos*, not intoxicated, a stone believed to guard its wearer against drunkenness. Whether one reads that literally or as metaphor, the association endures — Amethyst is the sober mind, the clear head, the still surface on which vision can appear.

## Properties

Amethyst governs the upper reaches of the [chakra](/knowledge/chakras) system, opening the [third eye](/knowledge/chakras/ajna) to intuition and quieting the noise that keeps it closed. It is the great calming stone — an antidote to anxiety, overthinking, and the compulsions that pull the mind away from itself. Long associated with sobriety in every sense, it steadies those working to release a craving, and offers a gentle but real spiritual protection, dissolving lower energies before they take root.

## In Practice

Placed beneath the pillow or on the nightstand, Amethyst is the classic aid for restless sleep and vivid, meaningful dreams. Held in meditation it deepens stillness and sharpens inner sight. Many keep a cluster in a room to steady its atmosphere. Its purple fades in direct sun, so cleanse and charge it in moonlight rather than daylight.
