# Turquoise

> *The colour of sky held in stone, carried across five thousand years.*

---

## The Stone

Turquoise is one of the oldest gemstones known to humankind — worn by pharaohs and Persian kings, set into the sky-blue jewellery of the American Southwest, threaded through the burial goods of the ancient dead. Its colour is unmistakable: a robin’s-egg blue-green, sometimes even and pure, more often veined with the brown or black “matrix” of the host rock, a spidering web that jewellers prize as a signature of the earth it came from.

It is a soft, porous stone, and it lives with its wearer — absorbing oils, sunlight and time until the colour shifts and deepens. An old piece of turquoise carries the record of every hand that held it, which is part of why it has always felt less like an ornament than a companion.

## Properties

Turquoise is the stone of communication and healing, a master of the throat chakra. It loosens the tongue for honest, kind speech and helps the wearer say the true thing without cruelty. Across countless cultures it has served as a protective amulet — a guardian for travellers, warriors and riders — and it is still turned to as an ancient shield against harm. It is also a stone of wholeness, said to soothe the nerves and knit body and spirit into a calmer, more integrated whole.

## In Practice

Worn at the throat or wrist, it supports clear expression and stands guard through the day. It has long been given as a stone of friendship and protection, offered rather than bought for its fullest effect. Because it is soft and porous, it is kept away from perfume, oils and prolonged water, and cleansed only by gentle, dry means.
