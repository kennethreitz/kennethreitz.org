# Black Tourmaline

> *A wall is only unkind to what should not enter.*

---

## The Stone

Black Tourmaline, known to mineralogists as schorl, grows in long striated columns of the deepest opaque black, their surfaces grooved with fine parallel lines like the ridges of a Doric pillar. It has no shine to seduce and no colour to admire — it is simply, emphatically dark, and that plainness is the point. Rubbed or warmed, it becomes electrically charged, drawing dust and small particles toward itself, a physical curiosity the old naturalists noted long before anyone spoke of energy.

Of all the protective stones, this is the one practitioners reach for first. It does not perform. It stands at the threshold and holds the line, and it has been doing so in the traditions of many cultures for a very long time.

## Properties

Black Tourmaline is the premier stone of protection and shielding, forming a boundary around its bearer that deflects hostility, ill intent, and psychic intrusion. Anchored at the [root](/knowledge/chakras/muladhara), it grounds as it guards, transmuting dense or fearful energy into something cleaner and returning the nervous system to steadiness. It is especially valued for shielding against the invisible static of the modern world and for protecting sensitive people who absorb too much of what surrounds them.

## In Practice

Black Tourmaline is placed at the corners of a room or by the front door to seal a space, set near screens and devices, and carried in a pocket as a personal ward through crowded or draining days. It works beautifully paired with a grounding partner. Unlike most stones it is best cleansed by burial in earth or salt rather than water, returning what it has gathered to the ground.
