# Citrine

> *The sun does not apologise for shining.*

---

## The Stone

Citrine is quartz turned the colour of honey and morning light — a warm golden yellow that at its richest deepens toward amber and burnt orange. Natural citrine is genuinely uncommon, its colour the gift of trace iron, and it carries a glow that seems to come from within rather than merely reflect the room. Much of what is sold as citrine is heat-treated amethyst, browner and more abrupt in its tone; the true stone is subtler, a pale champagne warmth.

There is nothing shadowed about Citrine. It is the mineral kingdom’s expression of daylight and good cheer, a stone that seems constitutionally incapable of gloom. To hold it is to feel invited toward the brighter side of a thing.

## Properties

Citrine belongs to the [solar plexus](/knowledge/chakras/manipura), the seat of will, confidence, and personal power, and it warms that centre like sunlight warms the belly. It is famous as the merchant’s stone — carried for abundance, prosperity, and the manifestation of one’s aims — but its deeper gift is joy and the courage to act. It dispels self-doubt, feeds motivation, and, unusually among crystals, is said never to hold negative energy, so it never needs clearing of what it has absorbed.

## In Practice

Citrine is tucked into wallets, cash drawers, and workspaces to draw prosperity and steady the will. Held in meditation it kindles confidence and lifts a low mood; placed in the wealth corner of a home it is a favourite of feng shui practice. Its colour can fade in prolonged sun, so charge it briefly and store it out of harsh light.
