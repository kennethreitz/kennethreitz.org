# Tiger's Eye

> *The eye that watches from within — steady, golden, unafraid.*

---

## The Stone

Tiger's Eye is a quartz threaded with fibres of altered asbestos, and when it is cut and polished those fibres catch the light in a single moving band — a phenomenon called chatoyancy, the “cat’s eye” shimmer. Tilt it and a ribbon of gold slides across the golden-brown surface like a slitted pupil narrowing in the sun. The colours run from honey to deep bronze, banded and warm, with a satin sheen that feels older than fashion.

It has the reputation of a watchman’s stone. Roman soldiers carried it into battle, believing the ever-open eye would see danger coming and turn it aside. There is something in the hand-feel that agrees — dense, smooth, faintly warm, a stone that seems to be paying attention.

## Properties

Tiger’s Eye is the stone of grounded confidence. Where fear scatters the will, it gathers it back — steadying the nerve without inflating the ego, lending courage that keeps its feet on the ground. It sits squarely at the solar plexus, the seat of personal power, and is called on for willpower, decisiveness, and the kind of protection that comes not from armour but from clear sight. It marries fire’s drive to earth’s patience, which is why it suits people who burn bright but need to stay rooted.

## In Practice

Carried in the pocket, it is a talisman for a daunting day — an interview, a confrontation, a decision that has been circled for weeks. Held during meditation over the navel, it warms the resolve and quiets the second-guessing. Many keep it on a work desk as a small sentinel against distraction and doubt, turning it in the fingers when focus slips.
